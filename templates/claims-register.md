# Claims and evidence register

Maintain before persuasion. No patient identifiers or clinical records. A policy source establishes a rule, not a product's clinical benefit.

| Claim ID | Exact wording/visual implication + location | Type | Product/use/status; population/route | Evidence URL + section + exact excerpt | Published/effective/access date + zone | Study design/endpoint/timeframe/comparator | Fit, risks and limitations | Proposed disclosure/rewrite | REG/GUIDANCE/PLATFORM/HOUSE | Internal status | Clinical/legal reviewer & scope/status | Recheck trigger |

| C-001 | Fill with exact asset excerpt | Pending classification | UNKNOWN until verified | Evidence required | Actual dates only | If applicable | No inferred approval/effect | Draft pending review | Specify layer | BLOCKED until material evidence provided | PENDING; never invent approval | New product/use/state/evidence/policy/edit |

## Register rules
- Keep conflicting evidence, adverse effects and uncertainty; don't cherry-pick.
- Match exact formulation/route, population and claim magnitude/timeframe. Animal/COA/vendor evidence does not become human efficacy proof.
- Record operational proof separately (credentials, hours, service availability) and commercial proof separately (signed quote, quantity, price/billing terms).
- Record a qualitative claim's implied outcome too. “Restores youth” has an outcome even without numbers.
- All quantities/percentages need denominators/timeframes; “typical” needs a relevant population including attrition, not only successful completers.
- Evidence expiry/review date is a house control, not a guarantee the claim remains safe until then.
