# Industry deep dive — offers, pricing, competitors, LegitScript ads

**Access date:** 2026-11-07 (America/New_York)  
**Scope:** US adult cash-pay telehealth (weight / hormones / peptides). Research only. No brand, domain, or ad account certified in this memo.  
**Skill pack:** `skills/clinic-legitscript-ads-gate`, `clinic-creative-healthcare`, `clinic-audience-targeting` (+ existing Meta/Google/clinical filters).

## Executive verdict
1. **Beachhead offer:** clinician-managed **weight** care with itemized membership + medication + labs (CaseMap Focus = decision receipt).  
2. **Paid Meta/YouTube for Rx:** need **LegitScript Healthcare Certification on your root domain** + **platform-specific authorization** (Meta Rx onboarding; Google healthcare certification). LS alone is not enough.  
3. **Experimental peptides as hero ads:** treat as **BLOCKED** for Meta/Google prescription paths until independent lawful + platform-eligible status is proven.  
4. **Competitors already compete on membership clarity and (often) before/after social proof**—do not copy risky creatives; win on honest receipts and process.

## 2. Competitor pricing snapshot (public pages, 2026-11-07)

### Ro Body (weight)
Source: https://ro.co/weight-loss/pricing/  
- Membership: **$39 first month** (refunded if ineligible for GLP-1s); then **$149/mo** monthly or **as low as $74/mo** annual prepaid.  
- Medication **separate**; cash-pay GLP-1 prices marketed to match manufacturer direct programs (e.g. Wegovy/Foundayo pill starts marketed at **$149/mo** first dose tiers on page).  
- Strong published renewal + ineligibility refund posture.

### Mochi Health
Source: https://joinmochi.com/  
- Health membership **$79/mo** (promo first month $0 on 1-month plan in some states); **$49/mo** on 12-month plan.  
- Insurance path “from $17/mo” membership variant advertised.  
- Meds self-pay: branded injectables (Wegovy from ~$199, Zepbound from ~$299, Foundayo from ~$149); compounded sema from ~$60, tirz from ~$90; labs from ~$26.  
- Heavy before/after UGC on homepage—**do not treat competitor ads as legal/platform safe harbor**.

### Adjacent benchmarks (prior research Nov 7)
- **Hone:** Basic ~$25/mo + meds; Premium ~$155/mo + meds.  
- **Evernow:** menopause memberships from ~$35/mo or visit packs.  
- Exact checkout/state restrictions always re-verify before quoting in ads.

### Our economics attach (MyDose Studio public)
- **$499/mo** incl. 50 active + **$15**/extra; meds/consults/labs/shipping pass-through unpublished.  
- Not a patient-facing price—fold into unit economics, not landing “from” claims.

## 4. Meta vs Google/YouTube with LegitScript

### Meta (Drugs & Pharmaceuticals + Health & Wellness)
Accessed 2026-11-07.

| | General telehealth service ads | Prescription-drug promoting ads |
|---|---|---|
| LS + Meta auth | Not required for “telehealth generally” per policy text | Required (LS for telehealth/pharmacy; manufacturer may use Meta internal review) |
| Geo | Broader (still follow other policies) | US / Canada / NZ; only certified countries |
| Age | Often 18+ for weight/sexual/reproductive | **18+** mandatory for Rx |
| Disclaimer | Context-dependent | Consult professional / valid prescription |
| Creative hazards | Personal attributes; appearance shame; fat pinch; cure claims; clickbait timelines | Same + Rx onboarding |

Weight-loss **products requiring prescription** → Drugs & Pharmaceuticals, not only Health & Wellness.

### Google Ads / paid YouTube (US)
Accessed 2026-11-07 from Healthcare & medicines country table.

- Telemedicine: **LegitScript Healthcare Merchant Certification** required.  
- **Also** Google healthcare certification.  
- Online pharmacies: LS **or** NABP digital/.pharmacy.  
- Restricts online prescribing/dispensing/sale promotion; drug terms restricted; speculative/experimental treatments generally disallowed; unapproved lists apply.  
- Sensitive health → **no** Customer Match / your-data remarketing / lookalikes / audience expansion; predefined audiences OK subject to rules.

**Organic YouTube** monetization/community guidelines ≠ paid Ads approval.

### Practical “what can we run?” ladder
1. **Pre-cert:** organic education, SEO, email (non-PHI), unpaid social—still clinical/claims review; no Rx checkout cloak.  
2. **Post-LS + Google cert:** Search/Demand Gen/YT for eligible telemedicine services; careful drug terms.  
3. **Post-LS + Meta Rx auth:** Meta ads that name/promote Rx products with required targeting + disclaimer.  
4. **Never assume:** compounded/experimental peptide heroes are certifiable—prove molecule-by-molecule.
