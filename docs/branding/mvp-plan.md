## MVP marketing
**Site (bounded):** Home · How care works · One service path (after niche/partner) · Costs & receipts · Practice & privacy · Start evaluation → separate clinical origin.

**Channels:** Own education first. Search/Meta/YouTube only after platform/healthcare refresh. Block patient-list / health enhanced conversions by default. MyDose Studio: no SMS sequences; no signup/purchase events to ads.

**Day-0 owned assets:** domain/DNS, logo+tokens, claims register, decision-receipt template, privacy/practice identity; clinical intake stays partner-owned with no ad pixels.
