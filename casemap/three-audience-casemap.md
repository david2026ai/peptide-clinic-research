## Pick: Recovery & performance
The only map where Us leads. Focus = plain-language protocol guidance (A3) plus supply continuity and regulatory honesty (A5). There is no Tier-1 breaker; the advantage is the timing window after the Oct 28 HHS policy, before Hims ships BPC-157.

Speakable: "Prescribed, single-ingredient recovery peptides from a named pharmacy, explained plainly, with a straight answer on what happens if the rules change."

## Biggest risk
The pick has the weakest paid-ad eligibility. Ads must sell clinician-guided recovery care without naming BPC-157 or TB-500. Weight care has the better channel fit, but it loses the map unless MyDose enables a brand GLP-1 (Foundayo).

## Pricing (research-based, hypothesis)
RUO vials: $30–80. Telehealth programs: $180–600/mo, mid-market ~$200–300. In-person: ~$445 per cycle. MyDose cost floor: $145–165/mo.
Recommended: $229–249/mo. Low-mid market, justified by guidance. MyDose's suggested $179 leaves about $29/mo above the cost floor before ad spend.

## Give-ups
Blends and stacks; a price war with RUO vendors; drug-named ads; non-covered peptides on this line; outcome claims.

## Re-run when
Hims launches BPC-157; HHS revisits the policy; MyDose adds a brand GLP-1; first pilot CAC and retention data arrive.
