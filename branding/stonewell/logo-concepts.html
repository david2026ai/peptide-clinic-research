Creative Brief

Target Audience

  • US adults 42–62, paying cash
  • HHI $175K+ — executives, founders, physicians
  • Already buy: wearables, nutrition coaches, sleep labs
  • Pain: reactive medicine doesn't optimize
  • Reads: Attia, Huberman, Rhonda Patrick
  • Media: YouTube + Meta paid only. No organic.

Brand Territory

  • Permanence over trend
  • Evidence over hype
  • Plan over catalog
  • Clinician-guided, not DIY supplements
  • Investment-grade health thinking
  • Straight answers — no AI wellness theater

Hard No's

  • Biohacking aesthetics (neon, circuit motifs)
  • Purple-indigo AI default palette
  • Cream + terracotta wellness cliché
  • Anti-aging / reverse aging claims
  • Compound names in ads (NAD+, peptides)
  • Before/after photos or testimonial results

Paid Video Hooks (Phase 1)

  • "Most executives optimize everything except their biology"
  • "At 48 I had more money than energy"
  • "Your labs say you're fine. We read them differently."
  • "If you're serious about the next 30 years…"
  • No drug names. No outcome claims. Service-led.

Offer Architecture

  • Care evaluation + annual membership — launch offer
  • Labs as separate line items ("decision receipt")
  • 12-month clinician plan — the core deliverable
  • Prescriptions only if clinically appropriate
  • Price range: $229–$349/mo (cost floor: ~$135)

LegitScript Path

  • Apply LS Healthcare Cert per domain → ~4 wks
  • Phase 1: service-level ads (no cert needed)
  • Phase 2: Meta Rx auth + Google Healthcare Cert
  • Unlocks: TRT/hormone service ads, drug-adjacent KWs
  • BLOCKED always: patient lists, health lookalikes
