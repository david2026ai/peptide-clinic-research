# Scope and non-negotiable operating contract

Applies to this draft pack and its future local copies. Author content and audits only unless a separate authorized task explicitly permits a specific external action. These documents never grant account access, credential access, publication, messaging, spend, repository creation, commits, or global skill changes.

## Required context and routing
Read `templates/clinic-context.md` and available project context first. Record service, patient segment, actual patient-location states/countries, treating entity/clinical lead, ownership model, prescriber/pharmacy authority, product/formulation/route, evidence/approval status, visit format, prices, traffic channel, data flows and requested output. No client segment or jurisdiction is preselected. Minors require a separate specialist/legal design; this pack's default is adult U.S. services, not an assertion of a universal legal age rule.

Read shared references and the relevant skill(s). Website: copy + editing. Video: creative workflow. All marketing then receives the clinical filter, applicable platform filter, client/state review and final release gate. In a combined report, retain distinct findings for each layer.

## Rule taxonomy
- **REG:** statute/regulation or operative legal requirement; cite jurisdiction and controlling authority. Legal applicability belongs to counsel, not this pack.
- **GUIDANCE:** regulator/professional guidance or requested/draft policy; cite status. Do not present it as a binding rule or FDA approval.
- **PLATFORM:** current platform policy/certification/destination/targeting rule. It can be stricter than law and changes independently.
- **HOUSE:** deliberately conservative business/editorial rule. State it as such; don't claim the law/platform prohibits all alternatives.
Resolve conflicts by satisfying every applicable layer; a client cannot waive law or platform rules. Never fabricate a controlling citation. Missing material evidence or unavailable current source blocks the affected release decision, not all neutral research.

## Internal verdicts and external review
- **PASS:** no issue found within the specified scope and supplied evidence, as of a stated date. Not legal clearance or platform prediction.
- **REVISE:** remediable copy/visual/disclosure problem with an exact excerpt and proposed rewrite.
- **BLOCKED:** material evidence/authority/source/consent missing, impermissible product/channel, unresolved data leakage or structural concern. A softer adjective cannot fix it.
Overall status takes the most restrictive unresolved finding. External tracks: clinical, jurisdictional legal, privacy/security, platform certification, client policy, owner publication authorization; each is PENDING / DOCUMENTED / NOT REQUIRED WITH REASON. Never invent reviewer names, signatures, approval or certification. Documents start PENDING. Even documented review applies only to that version, placement and jurisdiction.

## Publication and source gates
Refresh relevant primary sources before each substantive audit and again before release; log actual access date, effective date, source type and exact supporting excerpt. A cached page is an observation, not certainty about an upcoming rule. Check product label, shortage/compounding status, DEA dates, state board rules and platform healthcare/audience/data policies. Flag source failures precisely. Do not claim compliance from a snippet or substitute a pharmacy blog for the official rule. Draft conditionally when unknown; never label “ready to launch.”

## Patient-first house rules
No guaranteed eligibility/prescription, diagnosis by quiz, therapeutic guarantee, no-risk drug claim, fabricated patient/clinician, RUO-to-human funnel or pressure to bypass medical review. No clinician incentives/targets based on prescribing conversion. No dosing advice generated for consumers. Legitimate factual, properly sourced education is allowed: naming menopause, TRT, obesity or a medicine is not itself a violation. Identify when education becomes product promotion through claims, links, pricing or context.

No default patient tracking imports. Read `references/privacy-measurement.md`; no patient lists, health-derived audiences, sensitive enhanced conversions, clinical session replay or partner lead auctions. Do not weaken security controls for attribution. Use synthetic test data; no patient identifiers/records in this folder or general creative tools.

## Output standard
Deliver exact asset excerpts, reason, taxonomy, source/date, rewrite, internal status and remaining human decisions using `templates/audit-report.md`. Re-audit the full assembled asset after changes, including the destination and disclosures. Translations, subtitles, crops, thumbnails and generated visuals are new reviewable variants. Never promise a rewrite is compliant merely because it passed a text filter.
