## Recommendation and assumptions
Working assumption: U.S. adult cash-pay clinic. Initial states, budget, clinical partner, ownership eligibility, existing audience and physical/hybrid/virtual preference were **unknown**. Recommendation for this project: test clinician-led menopause care in one to three states, approved options first when appropriate, with local referrals and continuity. Not a universal rule: a well-supported TRT or obesity niche could warrant another decision after the same research gates.

One service first, not a combined “optimize everything” catalog. Treating practice retains genuine clinical control; a PC/MSO arrangement is counsel-designed, not an automatic fix. Direct-response research/creative skills can support education and acquisition, not establish clinical capability. Do not copy legal-lead auctions, coercive injury hooks or patient attribution assumptions.

## Four distinct service businesses
- **TRT:** real diagnosis/evaluation and ongoing monitoring, fertility implications, Schedule III/DEA continuity. A fatigue quiz is not a diagnosis; no universal high-normal optimization target. Separate clinical/operating gate before launch.
- **Menopause:** symptom/risk-led hormonal/nonhormonal options and referral; systemic/local treatments differ. No claim natural/bioidentical means safer, no automatic lab-balance funnel. Approved choices preferred over routine compounds when available.
- **Obesity/GLP-1:** longitudinal eligible care, exact brand/formulation indication, affordability/adverse-event/support planning. Medication cost separate; no automatic compounded-copy subsidy.
- **Experimental peptides:** exclude from launch revenue/acquisition. Product/route-specific evidence and lawful access must be reviewed independently. October 2026 draft HHS discretion is not approval, efficacy or blanket permission for catalog blends.

Current FDA labeling changes, compounding status, the December 31, 2026 DEA expiry, state ownership/licensure, cash-pay estimate duties and privacy questions require refresh. See `../SOURCES.md` and references; this export is not a legal assurance.

## Brand and experience
Positioning hypothesis: evidence-informed midlife care with a clinician who explains options and stays involved. Process promise: **Clear options. Transparent costs. A plan reviewed with a licensed clinician.** Staff and prove named-team continuity, follow-up, response times, referral and escalation before promising them.

Voice: calm, adult, plain, nonjudgmental; precise about uncertainty. Visuals: warm editorial clinical design, real credentialed clinicians, accessible contrast and ordinary life-stage images; no vials-as-hero, miracle lab coats or body transformations. Neutral master brand with service subpages; don't anchor the master brand on experimental peptides.

Naming territories only (not trademark/domain/state-name cleared): clarity/navigation (“Northline Midlife,” “Clearpath Midlife Care”); continuity (“Common Ground Midlife,” “Steadyline Health”); life stage (“Midlife Practice,” “Next Chapter Hormone Care”).

Website: Home → service → evaluation process → real clinical team → pricing/extras → states/eligibility → safety/alternatives → evidence → privacy/rights. Public service information separate from secure clinical intake/booking/portal. Care may properly result in no prescription or referral. Support inbox is not unlimited clinical availability.

## Proposed prices — unvalidated planning assumptions
- Initial evaluation $225; proposed 30–45 minutes and written plan, provider capacity contract required.
- Follow-up $125; proposed 15–25 minutes; actual frequency clinically determined.
- Later optional $69/month continuity after evaluation, up to four scheduled reviews/year plus **defined**, staffed messaging/coordination. Medication/labs extra; extra necessary care priced/accessed transparently; cancellation/refund specified.
- Later obesity-care test $99/month care fee, medication extra, only with capacity and affordability support.
- No launch TRT price (testosterone COGS unverified); no experimental peptide revenue.

$225 + four × $125 = $725/year in visits; versus Midi published analogous visit arithmetic $850, not a matched care protocol. $225 + 12 × $69 = $1,053/year; subscription not automatically cheaper and must earn its additional value. Inclusive Winona may cost less; no universal savings claim.

## Economic assumptions and stress
At $69/month, assumed clinical reserve 4 × $38/year ÷ 12 = $12.67 (vendor consult scope **not verified as adequate**), processing $2.76, support $10 and tech $5 → rounded contribution **$38.57/member/month** before CAC/fixed/vendor. Stress: clinical $35, support $15, tech $8, processing $2.76 → **$8.24**.

Assumed $4,000 monthly fixed cost: break-even roughly **104** base / **486** stress members, before acquisition. At 200 base members, rounded $7,714 contribution − $4,000 fixed = $3,714; minus illustrative $3,000 acquisition spend = **$714 before any vendor continuation/startup**. These are sensitivity cases, not forecasts. Build workload/retention/CAC evidence before subscription launch.

No verified monthly commodity hormone cost was substituted from an ambiguous workbook tier. $52.65/30-count gel and $1.50 enclomiphene tablet are source-specific units, not universal monthly care or testosterone costs. Illustrative $50,000 independent pilot envelope in the full Page excludes the $40,000 supplier build; not a quoted complete startup budget.

## Phases and gates
1. Select single niche/states/model and total cash-at-risk; name independent clinical lead and healthcare counsel.
2. Resolve entity/authority/records/fee flows, prescribing/pharmacy evidence, care/referral capacity, privacy and transparent patient terms before selling.
3. Prototype brand/service with real process proof; test informed demand, not guaranteed treatment.
4. Capped compliant acquisition pilot only after documented certification/review and separate publication/spend authorization. No patient-list retargeting or unsafe event uploads by default.
5. Scale from measured attended evaluation economics, clinically appropriate outcomes, support burden and actual retention. Experimental products are not a spreadsheet rescue.

Pending decisions: geography; service priority; clinical partner/entity role; budget; and vendor versus independent stack. No clinician/vendor/competitor was contacted and no account changes were made. These project recommendations do not override configurable reusable skills.
