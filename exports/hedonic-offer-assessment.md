## Economics — explicit scenario assumptions
Vendor equal four-product mix: rounded $31.43 new-order and $63.93 recurring-order contribution; recurring calculation assumes no repeat consult. At 100 new + 100 repeat orders, contribution $9,536 minus assumed 30 × $500 ads = **−$5,464/month**, before build, overhead and omitted care/logistics costs.

If continuation were already applicable (not established by a losing month), A minimum $2,000 would leave −$7,464; B $7,500 would leave −$12,964. Do not activate continuation in a projection without defining the profitability trigger.

At 100 new orders and $15,000 ads, assumed CAC = $150. Two-month contribution LTV = $31.43 + $63.93 = $95.36: **−$54.64 after CAC** before overhead. Six-month retention assumption gives $351.08 contribution before CAC, not demonstrated patient retention. Vendor first-year projections $70,848 conservative / $188,473 aggressive exclude ads/CAC and continuation. Subtracting $40,000 build and eleven assumed full $15,000 ad months leaves −$134,152 / −$16,527 before further costs; this is an illustrative stress case, not vendor forecast or exact warm-up spend.
