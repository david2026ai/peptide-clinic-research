## 5. Demand OS SAM (dual — lead **and** SaaS)

**Rule:** Keep **both** bands on the page. GTM choice (A / B / C) does not delete the other SAM.

### 5.1 Lead-sale SAM

```text
Lead_SAM_GMV ≈ N_buyers × (leads_per_mo × RPL) × 12
  — or —
Lead_SAM_GMV ≈ N_buyers × monthly_lead_wallet × 12

N_buyers = clinics that will buy exclusive/instant leads (subset of ICP universe)
```

| Scenario | N buyable clinics | Avg lead spend / mo | Gross lead GMV / yr | When true |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Conservative** | 400 | $4,000 | **~$19M** | Tight HQ buyers; health-ad constraints |
| **Base** | 1,000 | $6,000 | **~$72M** | Mid take of ICP; media scales |
| **Optimistic** | 2,000 | $8,000 | **~$192M** | Broad ND/hormone/peptide adoption |
| **Speakable lead SAM band** | **500–2,000** | **$3–10k** | **~$40–200M+** | Prefer range; contribution ≪ GMV |

**Alternate unit view:** 800 buyers × 20 exclusive inquiries/mo × **$250** RPL × 12 ≈ **~$48M** GMV.

**Contribution habit:** aim ~**⅓** after media + rejects (SYM GM discipline) ⇒ **~$15–70M** contribution on the speakable GMV range (**judgment**). Health ads often cost more than PI — calibrate in cells.

**Buyable vs universe:** **N_buyers ≪ N_ICP** (many referral-only / under-spend) — hence **500–2,000**, not 1.5–4k.

Demand source can be **nationwide YouTube** (SYM playbook) or other paid channels; exclusivity still scopes per cell.

### 5.2 SaaS SAM (paid)

```text
SaaS_SAM_ARR ≈ N_ICP × ARPU_mo × 12
  N_ICP   = deduped small cash-pay longevity / peptide / hormone / ND–functional (WTP for software)
  ARPU_mo = paid Demand OS subscription (not clinic OS)
```

| Scenario | N clinics | ARPU / mo | ARR | When true |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Conservative** | 1,200 | $1,500 | **~$22M** | Tight WTP; simple CRM only |
| **Base** | 2,000 | $2,500 | **~$60M** | Mid universe; Pro lands |
| **Optimistic** | 3,500 | $3,500 | **~$147M** | Broad functional/ND adopt |
| **Speakable SaaS SAM band** | **1,500–3,500** | **$1–5k** | **~$25–120M** | Prefer range |

**Non–tech-savvy:** weight toward **$1–2.5k/mo** + simple UX; complex “clinic OS” kills WTP.

### 5.3 GTM option C — YouTube + Free SaaS (one path, not the only)

| Element | Spec |
|---|---|
| **Motion** | Nationwide YouTube → **instant lead sale**; Demand OS **free** (or free-with-lead-buyer) |

### 5.4 What to say on slides

1. **Clinic universe ~1.5–4k** (dedicated longevity ~500–800).  
2. **Lead SAM ~$40–200M+ GMV** · **SaaS SAM ~$25–120M ARR** — both valid.
