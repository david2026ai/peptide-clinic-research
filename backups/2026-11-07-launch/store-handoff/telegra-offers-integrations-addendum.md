## What it is (observed)

**Telegra** (domain `telegramd.com` — **not** the Telegram messenger) markets a white-label **telehealth commerce + clinical ops** stack: intake → cart/checkout → provider review → pharmacy routing → patient portal/app → ops “Command Center,” plus API/webhooks.

Homepage claims (unverified): 200+ health brands; 5M+ patient journeys/year; 50-state coverage; “100% HIPAA Compliant”; LegitScript icon in footer (assistance listed on pricing — **not** independent proof of a live certified domain).

**Product verticals advertised:** weight loss (GLP-1 / compounded formulations claimed), longevity, men’s TRT, women’s HRT, sexual health, dermatology, hair loss, STD; **Peptides = “Coming Soon.”** FAQ: no Category 2 research-only peptide offer yet; “the day they are [on FDA bulk list], we’ll be live” — treat as marketing posture, not legal clearance.

## Published commercial offer (pricing page)

| Plan | Published platform fee | Onboarding | Notable inclusions (vendor list) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Plus** | **$2,999 / month** | **$5,000** or 3 × $1,750/mo | Unlimited Command Center seats; white-label portal; claimed 50-state providers; **3** compounding pharmacy partners; API/webhooks; own processing account; LegitScript assistance; async + sync care; ID verify **$1**/patient/year |
| **Pro** | **$5,999 / month** | **$10,000** or 3 × $3,500/mo | Everything in Plus + white-label **iOS/Android** app; **full** pharmacy network + **BYOP** (one-time integration fee); custom/branded labs & supplements; ID verify **$0.75**/patient/year; quarterly website legal audit |

**FAQ economics claims (unverified):** fixed fee, **not** revenue share; brand sets prices and keeps margin; Pro “pays for the difference” at ~**600 patients** (their arithmetic — not independent). Onboarding timeline listed **21 days** (FAQ also says 2–3 weeks; LegitScript/site readiness called the usual variable).

**Not published on reviewed pages:** per-visit clinical fees, drug COGS, pharmacy fill fees, shipping, lab pass-throughs, SMS/email unit costs beyond ID verify, BYOP integration dollar amount, termination fees, MSA/BAA text, full pharmacy roster (FAQ: “ask us for the full list”; claims **15** 503A partners).
