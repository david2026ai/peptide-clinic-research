Billing & Payments
Connect Stripe, read your monthly bill, manage payment methods, and review your clinic's billing history.
The
Billing & Payments
page is where you connect Stripe, see what your clinic has been charged, manage saved cards, and download billing records.
Where to find it
Select the gear menu, then select
Billing
.
Billing & Payments overview
Connect Stripe
The
Stripe Payment
card at the top of the
Overview
tab shows your connection status. Until payment setup is complete, the card reads
Setup incomplete
, and an amber banner — "Finish Stripe setup to start accepting payments" — appears across every page of the portal.
Open the Stripe Payment card
From the gear menu, select
Billing
and find the
Stripe Payment
card on the
Overview
tab.
Finish what Stripe needs
Select
Resolve in Stripe →
on the card to complete any outstanding requirements. From anywhere in the portal, you can also select
Continue setup
on the amber banner.
Disconnect if you need to
Select
Disconnect
on the
Stripe Payment
card to remove the connection.
Payment setup is also one of the four items on your Getting Started checklist — "Add payment method — Required to start receiving payments from patients." See
Clinic Setup
.
Read your bill
Two figures on the
Overview
tab summarize your charges:
Figure
What it shows
Total Charged This Month
What your clinic has been charged so far this month
Upcoming Bill
Your next charge — select
View breakdown
to see what makes it up
Payment methods
The
Payment Methods
section holds the cards on file for your clinic. Select
Add New Card
to save a new card.
Billing history
The
Billing History
section lists every charge on your account. Use
Search
to find a specific transaction, or select
Download Report
to export the list.
Column
What it shows
Date
When the charge happened
Amount Charged
The amount billed
Type
The kind of transaction — for example,
Account top-up
Description
A description of the transaction
For example, a $2.00
Account top-up
appears with a Stripe charge reference. Each row also has Row actions.
Your wallet balance is separate from billing. Top-ups appear here as charges, but your current balance and its full history live on the
Account Balance
page — opened from the
Balance
chip in the header on every page.
