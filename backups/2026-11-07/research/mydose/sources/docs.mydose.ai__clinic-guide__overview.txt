Platform Overview
Understand what MyDose.ai does and how your clinic runs on it day to day.
MyDose.ai is a practice management platform built for clinics offering longevity therapy. It connects your clinical team with patients through a single system that handles patient onboarding, treatment planning, consultations, pharmacy fulfillment, and adherence tracking.
Where to find it
Sign in at
app.mydose.ai
. Your team manages everything from the clinic
Dashboard
; patients interact through their own portal to review treatment plans, log doses, and message your team.
MyDose clinic dashboard
The sidebar puts
Dashboard
,

, and
Calendar
at the top, then groups the rest into
CARE
(Treatment Plans, Patients, Messages),
CLINIC
(Services, Library), and
OPERATIONS

Navigating the portal

Use the
Live mode / Demo mode

How the core loop works
Most day-to-day work follows one loop:
Start by
adding a patient
, then
assign a package
or
create a treatment plan
. Once the patient completes their intake,
review and approve it
to activate treatment.
Finish account setup
New accounts see a
GETTING STARTED
card on the Dashboard — "Finish account setup to start treating patients" — with four required items:
Verify your email
Required to activate your account.
Add payment method
Required to start receiving payments from patients.
Verify medical license
Required to prescribe and activate treatments.
Set up calendar availability
Required to receive consultation bookings.
The
Viva
setup assistant (the blue sparkles button in the bottom-right) walks you through these steps and resumes at your first incomplete one. See
Clinic setup
for the full walkthrough.
How roles work
Role
Default access
Owner
Full access to everything, including admin settings and team management.
Doctor
All prescriber permissions by default — prescribe, adjust and approve treatments, approve intakes, order labs. Can be narrowed per member.
Staff
Schedule and cancel appointments, add and manage patients, order bloodwork. No prescribing or approvals by default.
Permissions are configured in
Settings → User Management
— see
Team management
and
Roles & permissions
for details.
Whitelabel clinic accounts see a reduced portal: clinical items in the sidebar are disabled.
Key terms
Term
Meaning
Services
The offerings your clinic makes available to patients, managed from
Services
in the sidebar.
Treatment Plan
The set of treatments a patient is assigned — created by your team and approved by a prescriber before it becomes active.
Intake
The medical questionnaire a patient completes before a prescriber can approve their treatment.
Consultation Type
A bookable appointment format patients can schedule against your calendar availability.
