Choose your products
Turn on the MyDose products your store sells and check how they look.
Your store sells products from the MyDose catalog. MyDose owns the catalog and the prices. You choose which products appear in your store.
Before you start
Finish your
brand kit
so product pages pick up your look.
Read
Set prices
so you know what you control.
Choose products
Open Products
In your dashboard sidebar, select
Products
. You can also reach this from the
Products
step of the setup wizard.
Select what you sell
Turn on each product you want in your store. Turn off any you do not want to offer.
Review each listing
Open a listing to see what patients will see. Check the name, description and price shown.
Check your store
Open
Storefronts
, select your store and
Edit store
. Look at the product pages and the product grid.
What you'll see
Products page with suggested prices and the Add all button
Selected products show on your store's product grid and each gets its own page. Home shows
Products chosen
on your Setup guide.
What you can change
In the store editor you can change the imagery, wording and search settings around a product. Prices appear read-only there.
You cannot change who receives a product. Independent licensed providers review each patient's intake and decide whether to prescribe. Medical services provided by independent licensed providers.
What happens next
Build the rest of your pages in
Edit your store
. Then run through your store in
test mode
.
Common problems
Symptom
Fix
A product I expect is missing
The product may not be offered to your brand. Email
support@mydose.ai
with the product name.
A product shows on my grid but I want it gone
Turn it off in
Products
, then check the grid in the editor.
The price in the editor will not change
Prices are read-only in the editor. See
Set prices
.
I added a product but my checklist does not tick
Reload Home. If it still does not tick, email
support@mydose.ai
.
Patients see old product wording
Select
Publish
on the page. Drafts do not change the live store.
Do not name medications in headlines or ads, and do not promise results. See
Compliance for brand owners
.
