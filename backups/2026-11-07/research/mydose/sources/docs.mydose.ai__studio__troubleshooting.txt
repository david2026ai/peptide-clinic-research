Studio troubleshooting
Fixes for sign-in, verification, payouts, product, page, domain, billing and AI problems.
Find your symptom, try the fix, and email
support@mydose.ai
if it does not clear. Include your store name and what you see on screen. Do not send patient information by email.
Before you start
Note which page you were on and the exact message you saw. It makes support faster.
Account and sign-in
I did not get my verification code
Check spam. Request a new code from the sign-in screen. Use the same email you signed up with. If it still does not arrive, email
support@mydose.ai
.
I cannot sign in
Use the email you signed up with at studio.mydose.ai. If a reset does not work, email
support@mydose.ai
.
An invite did not arrive
Check spam, then resend it from
Team
.
Going live
The Go live button is missing
It only shows while a store is in test mode. Check the store's status under
Storefronts
.
Go live is blocked
The first go-live needs payouts to be ready. Open
Payouts
and finish the Stripe steps.
My store shows a test banner
It shows while the store is in test mode. It goes when the store is live.
Pages and editing
I do not see my edits
Select
Publish
. Saving a draft does not change your live store. Clear your browser cache and reload.
A template replaced my pages
Applying a template pack replaces Home, About and Contact. Use
History
on each page to restore a version as a new draft, then
Publish
.
I deleted a page
Open
Trash
and restore it.
Products and prices
I cannot find the product I want
Open
Products
and turn it on. If it is not listed, email
support@mydose.ai
with the name.
A price looks wrong
Prices are MyDose-owned and read-only in the editor. Email
support@mydose.ai
with the product.
Payouts and billing
Payouts will not connect
Open
Payouts
and go through the Stripe steps again. Check bank details. If Stripe shows a problem you cannot clear, email
support@mydose.ai
.
I was charged and expected not to be
Email
support@mydose.ai
with the date.
A payment failed
Update your payment method in
Billing
.
AI assistants
The assistant cannot write
You need to be an Owner or Admin and the connection must not be read-only. See
Build with an AI assistant
.
A confirm code expired
Codes last 5 minutes. Ask again and repeat the request.
Patients
I cannot read a patient message
Support
shows who wrote and when, not the text. Use
Open MyDose
to read and reply.
A patient asks a medical question
Do not answer. Send them to the care team through the patient portal.
Wording
I am not sure what my page wording should say
Keep it to what your programs are. Do not give medical advice, name medications or promise results. See
Compliance for brand owners
.
What you'll see
Most problems clear with one of the fixes above. Many show a message at the top of the page.
What happens next
If you are still stuck, email
support@mydose.ai
. Email is the only support channel.
