Common questions
What does MyDose provide to run a telehealth business?
MyDose connects patient intake, consultations, clinical programs, lab tracking, prescriptions, pharmacy fulfillment and payments. A clinic portal and patient app support the process from enrollment through ongoing treatment.
What is the difference between Launch, Studio and Enterprise?
Studio is self-service: build your own brand in a free sandbox. Enterprise is sales-led: migrations, custom builds and multi-brand groups under one contract.
What do the products cost?
Launch is $0 to your business: no setup, activation or monthly fee. Studio is free until your first patient, then $499 a month with 50 active patients included and $15 per active patient above that. Enterprise pricing is scoped with our team. Medication is always billed at pharmacy cost.
Does a business need its own practitioners?
No. Licensed MyDose providers review intake, make the treatment decision and prescribe. Clinics with their own practitioners can discuss custom arrangements under Enterprise.
Can an existing clinic keep its current systems and pharmacy relationships?
Enterprise covers migrations and custom requirements, including existing pharmacy relationships where they meet MyDose’s 503A and licensing standards. Scope is agreed during discovery.
How do patients access their treatment information?
Patients use the patient portal or app to view treatment plans, message their provider, track shipments and manage refills, renewals and cancellations.
How is patient information protected?
Patient data is encrypted in transit and at rest with role-based access, secure messaging and automatic backups. Consent, signatures and treatment changes are logged and timestamped.
Can the platform be explored before getting started?
Yes. Book a demo and we’ll walk you through the platform, or create a free account to look around the clinic portal.
