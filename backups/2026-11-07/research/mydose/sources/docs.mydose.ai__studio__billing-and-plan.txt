Billing and your plan
What Studio costs, when billing starts, and how the per-patient charge works.
Studio costs nothing until your first patient. Then it is one monthly plan. This page explains each number.
The short version
$0 until your first patient.
Then $499 a month, with 50 active patients included.
$15 per active patient above 50. This is taken from your payouts. It is not charged to your card.
No setup fee.
When billing starts
You build, preview and test. Test mode does not start billing. Your plan starts with your first live patient purchase.
What counts as an active patient
An active patient is a patient whose live plan payment covers any part of the billing month. Each patient is counted once per month. Test orders do not count.
Above 50 active patients
Each active patient above 50 in a month adds $15 for that month. MyDose takes the amount from your payouts, oldest month first. It is not billed to your card. If a payout is fully used up, no transfer is made for it.

What the plan covers
The plan includes your storefront, the patient portal, intake, e-consent and checkout, access to independent licensed providers, and a partner pharmacy. You do not need your own doctors or a medical license.
Medication and consults
Medication and provider consults pass through at cost. MyDose does not add a markup. The difference between your price and those costs is yours.
