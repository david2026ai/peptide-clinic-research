MyDose Approved Packages
Create a package from treatments pre-approved by MyDose doctors — search the catalog, pick a treatment, and continue with limited customization.
MyDose Approved lets you build a package from treatments pre-approved by MyDose doctors.
What is MyDose Approved?
When you select
Create Package
on the Services page, the
New Package
dialog offers
MyDose Approved
, described as: "Choose from pre-approved treatments by MyDose doctors with limited customization."
Choose it when speed and pre-vetted clinical content matter more than full control. If you need to define your own categories, visit requirements, medication, treatment schedule, and pricing, build a
Custom Package
instead.

Where to find it
Go to
Services
, select
Create Package
, choose
MyDose Approved
in the
New Package
dialog, then select
Continue
. You can also open
/services/mydose-approved
directly.
MyDose Approved treatment picker
Pick a treatment
The
Set treatment details
screen shows the catalog of pre-approved treatments.
Search or filter the catalog
Use the search box or the
All categories
,
Any price
, and
All forms
filters to narrow the list.
Review the treatment cards
Each card shows the treatment name, its category tags, "from $X.XX/month" pricing, and the underlying product. In the demo clinic, examples include
Tirzepatide + B12
from $249.00/month,
Semaglutide + B12
from $300.00/month, and
NAD+ Injectable
from $262.50/month.
Select a treatment
Select the card for the treatment you want.
Continue
stays disabled until a treatment is selected.
Continue or exit
Select
Continue
to proceed with the selected treatment, or
Exit
to leave the picker.
Availability
Whether MyDose Approved is available is controlled by the
MyDose Approved
toggle in Services Settings — open
Services
, select
Settings
, and switch the toggle. See the
Services overview
for the rest of the Services Settings options.
Pricing shown in the treatment picker is the demo clinic's marketplace pricing. Your clinic's rates may differ.
