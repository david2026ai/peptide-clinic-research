Calendar & Appointments
Read your daily schedule, book appointments for existing or new patients, and manage consultation types from the Calendar.
The
Calendar
is where you stay on top of your schedule — see the day's booked sessions, jump between dates, book new appointments, and manage the consultation types patients can book.
Where to find it
Sign in at
app.mydose.ai
and select
Calendar
in the sidebar.
Calendar day view with mini month and consultation types
Set your availability before anything else — patients can't book consultations until your weekly hours are saved in
Calendar Settings
. "Set up calendar availability" is also one of the four required setup items in
clinic setup
.
Read the calendar
The main area is a day schedule (for example, "Today, July 22, 2026") with
prev/next arrows
to move between days and a
time-zone chip
showing which time zone the schedule is displayed in. The right column holds a
mini month calendar
for jumping to any date, and the top bar has a
provider selector
— it defaults to the clinic, and you can switch it to view an individual provider's schedule.
When nothing is booked for the day, the schedule shows "No sessions scheduled for this day. Your schedule will appear here once you have booked sessions." along with two shortcut buttons:
Shortcut
What it does
Create Consultation Types
Define the bookable consultation types for your clinic
Set Availability
Set the hours you accept bookings
Book an appointment
Open the booking dialog
Select
Create Appointment
in the top bar. The
Book New Appointment
dialog opens.
Choose the patient
Pick the
Existing Patient
or
New Patient
tab, then use the
Select patient
dropdown to choose who the appointment is for.
Pick a consultation type
Select a type from the consultation-type picker — each option shows its duration and price.
Choose the prescriber and date
Select a prescriber from the prescriber dropdown, then enter the date (mm.dd.yyyy).
Pick a time slot
The time-slot dropdown stays disabled with "Select date and type first" until you've chosen both a date and a consultation type. Once it enables, select a time.
Create the booking
Select
Create Booking
to confirm, or
Cancel
to discard.
Book New Appointment dialog
Consultation types
The
Consultation Types
panel in the right column lists every bookable type — each shows a color dot, the name, duration, and price, plus a share icon. Select the
Manage
link (gear) to open
Calendar Settings
.
