Patient Health & Labs
Review a patient's weight and BMI charts, health conditions, diagnoses, questionnaire results, and medications, and order blood tests from the Lab results page.
The
Health
tab on a patient's profile brings together their vitals history, conditions, diagnoses, questionnaire results, medications, and labs in one place.
Where to find it
Open a patient's profile and select the
Health
tab.
Open the Patients list
Select
Patients
in the sidebar, then select the patient's row.
Select the Health tab
On the patient's profile, select
Health
(between
Overview
and
Treatments
).
Patient Health tab
Charts
At the top of the Health tab, two charts track the patient over time:
Weight history
— the patient's recorded weight entries.
BMI history
— the patient's BMI over time.
Use these to check progress at a glance before a consultation or when reviewing a treatment.
Conditions and diagnoses
Health Conditions
The
Health Conditions
section records ongoing conditions, split into two sub-tabs:
Chronic
— long-term conditions.
Allergies
— known allergies.
To record a new condition, select
Add condition
and fill in the details.
Diagnoses
The
Diagnoses
list shows diagnoses recorded for the patient, so prescribers can see the clinical picture alongside the charts and conditions.
Diagnostic Results
Diagnostic Results
lists the patient's submitted questionnaire results, shown by name and date (for example, "weight-loss 02/19/26"). To see the full intake submissions, use the
Records
tab — see
Review intakes
.
Medications
Current Medications
is a table of the medications the patient is taking. It shows "No medications" when nothing has been recorded.
To record one, select
Add medication
and enter the details.
Order labs
The
Labs
section links to the patient's Lab results page, where you can review results and order new bloodwork.
Open the patient's profile
Select
Patients
in the sidebar, then select the patient's row.
Go to Health
Select the
Health
tab.
Open the Lab results page
In the
Labs
section, select
View Labs
.
Order a test
Select
Order Blood Test
. Existing lab results are listed on this page; it shows an empty state if the patient has none yet.
Lab results page with Order Blood Test
Ordering labs requires the
Order bloodwork
permission. If you don't see the
Order Blood Test
button, ask a clinic admin to check your role — see
Roles & permissions
.
