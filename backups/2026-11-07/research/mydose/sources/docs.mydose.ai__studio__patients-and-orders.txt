Patients, orders and support
Where to see orders and patients, and who answers which questions.
After you go live, your day-to-day work is in four places: Orders, Patients, Subscriptions and Support.
Before you start
Your store is live. See
Test mode and going live
.
See your orders and patients
Open Orders
In the sidebar, select
Orders
. Each order shows its status. Use the source columns to see where it came from.
Open Patients
Select
Patients
to list the people who signed up on your storefront.
Open Subscriptions
Select
Subscriptions
for recurring orders.
Open Analytics and Marketing
Analytics
and
Marketing
show visits, sign-ups and orders by source, as totals without patient-level detail.
Some pages that show patient details require a team member with the right role. See
Team and roles
.
What the funnel counts
Analytics shows five stages: Started intake, Finished intake, Approved by a provider, Started treatment, and Still on treatment after 90 days.
Who answers questions
Question type
Who answers
Where
Medical (symptoms, dosing, side effects)
MyDose's care team
The patient portal. It does not appear in your dashboard.
Non-medical (orders, shipping, billing)
You
Read in
Support
, then reply in your MyDose clinic dashboard.
Open Support
In the sidebar, select
Support
. It lists who wrote, the date and whether it is unread. It never shows the message text.
Open MyDose
Use
Open MyDose
to sign in to your MyDose clinic dashboard in one click.
Reply there
Open the conversation under Messaging and reply. Replies are not sent from Studio.
Do not give patients medical advice. Send medical questions to the MyDose care team.
Emails your patients receive
Patients receive emails from the platform, branded with your store. Under
Notifications
you control two switches: the daily summary to you (on by default) and abandoned sign-up reminders to patients (off by default). Test mode sends no patient emails.
Your patients' address

<your-store>.mydose.store
for your storefront and
<your-store>-app.mydose.store
for the patient portal.

Send them
<your-store>-app.mydose.store
.

Check the test toggle in Analytics and that the store is live.

Follow your Refund Policy page.
