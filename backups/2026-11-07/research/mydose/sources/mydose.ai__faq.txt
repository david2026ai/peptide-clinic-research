Platform overview
What does MyDose provide to run a telehealth business?
MyDose connects patient intake, consultations, clinical programs, lab tracking, prescriptions, pharmacy fulfillment and payments. A clinic portal and patient app support the process from enrollment through ongoing treatment.
Can we customize MyDose for our specific protocols?
Yes. MyDose is built to be flexible. You can create custom protocol templates, adjust timelines, define check-in schedules, and customize intake forms. If you need something highly specific, our team can work with you to build custom workflows.
Do we have to switch from our current EMR?
Not necessarily. Some clinics use MyDose for longevity and functional medicine programs while keeping their existing EMR for insurance-based care. Others replace their EMR entirely. It depends on your practice mix.
Can the platform be explored before getting started?
Yes. Book a demo and we’ll walk you through the platform, or create a free account to look around the clinic portal.
Getting started
Can I keep my website?
Yes. Your website and funnel stay where they are. The patient portal runs under your brand, on your domain.
Pricing and fees
What does Launch cost my business?
Nothing. There is no setup fee, no activation fee and no monthly fee. You sign up, connect Stripe and share your code; MyDose earns on the care it delivers and you earn your program fee.
Does MyDose take a percentage of my Launch earnings?
No. You earn $75 per active protocol, per month, for every member on an active program, before any optional discount you choose to offer your members. It is paid to your own Stripe account.
Can I change what my members pay with Launch?
MyDose sets member pricing. You can choose to discount your portion of the earnings, which lowers what your members pay.
What does Studio cost?
The sandbox is free and nothing is billed until your first patient. Then it is $499 a month with 50 active patients included and $15 per active patient above that. Medication and provider consults pass through at cost.
What does Enterprise cost?
Enterprise pricing is scoped with our team, with medication at pharmacy cost.

Launch, Studio and Enterprise
Which product is right for me?
Launch if you have members and a front desk but no medical team: we run the care, you earn a program fee. Studio if you want to own the brand: build in a free sandbox, go live when you’re ready. Enterprise if you’re migrating an existing telehealth business or need custom terms.
Can I use my own brand?
Yes, with Studio or Enterprise. Studio is self-service on your own domain; Enterprise is set up to your agreed requirements. With Launch, members enroll with MyDose.
Is the Enterprise fee charged per product?
No. Enterprise is priced per active patient, per month, however many programs they are on. The rate is scoped with our team.
Clinical care and pharmacy
Does a business need its own practitioners?
No. Licensed MyDose providers review intake, make the treatment decision and prescribe. Clinics with their own practitioners can discuss custom arrangements under Enterprise.
Do I need a medical license?
No. Licensed providers make every clinical decision. You run the brand, the clients and the price.
Can an existing clinic keep its current systems and pharmacy relationships?
Enterprise covers migrations and custom requirements, including existing pharmacy relationships where they meet MyDose’s 503A and licensing standards. Scope is agreed during discovery.
What if our pharmacy doesn’t integrate with MyDose?
We work with a network of 503A U.S. pharmacies.
For patients
How do patients access their treatment information?
Patients use the patient portal or app to view treatment plans, message their provider, track shipments and manage refills, renewals and cancellations.
Is MyDose Health legal and safe?
MyDose Health operates as a telehealth service with licensed providers in all states we serve. All medications come from licensed 503A U.S. facilities.
Do I need a referral to join?
No. You can sign up directly through our website. However, if your gym, wellness center, or clinic works with MyDose, they may provide you with a referral link.
What if I already have a doctor?
MyDose Health providers can coordinate with your existing healthcare team, with your consent. We’re not replacing your primary care; we’re adding structured longevity protocols that most primary care practices don’t offer.
Can I use my insurance?
Most insurance plans don’t cover elective longevity or performance-focused care. Lab coverage varies by plan.
What if I experience side effects?
You can message your provider directly in the app.
How do I know if I’m qualified?
You complete a health intake form during signup.
What states are telemedicine offered in?
MyDose currently offers telemedicine services in all states except New Jersey.
Security and privacy
How is patient information protected?
Patient data is encrypted in transit and at rest with role-based access, secure messaging and automatic backups. Consent, signatures and treatment changes are logged and timestamped.
Is MyDose HIPAA compliant?
Yes. MyDose runs on HIPAA-compliant infrastructure with end-to-end encryption, secure messaging, role-based access controls, and automatic audit trails.
