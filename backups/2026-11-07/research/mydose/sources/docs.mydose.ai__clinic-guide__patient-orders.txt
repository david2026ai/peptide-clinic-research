Patient Orders
Read a patient's order history, unpaid orders, and current delivery from their profile, and start a new order with Order Products.
Every patient profile has an
Orders
tab that shows that patient's order history, any unpaid orders, and the status of their current delivery.
Where to find it
Open a patient's profile and select the
Orders
tab.
Patient profile Orders tab
If you're not sure how to reach a profile, see
Patient Profile
.
Read the Orders tab
The tab is split into three sections:
Section
What it shows
Order history
A table of the patient's past orders with columns
Order ID
,
Date
,
Amount
, and
Delivery status
. If the patient hasn't ordered yet, it reads "No orders history here yet".
Unpaid Orders
Orders awaiting payment, with columns
Order number
,
Due date
,
Amount
, and
Payment method
.
Current Delivery
The status of the patient's in-progress delivery.
For your clinic's own charges and billing history, see
Billing & Payments
.
Place an order for a patient
Ordering starts from the patient's profile, not the Orders tab itself.
Open the patient's profile
Find the patient and open their profile.
Select Order Products
Select the
Order Products
button in the profile header.
Follow the order flow
Follow the on-screen flow to complete the order.
Clinic-wide view
The patient profile only shows one patient's orders. To see and search orders across all patients, open
Orders Management
from the sidebar — see
Orders Management
.
