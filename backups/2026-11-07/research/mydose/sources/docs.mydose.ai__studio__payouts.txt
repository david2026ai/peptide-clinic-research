Payouts
Connect your bank through Stripe, read the Payouts page, and see what comes out.
Patient payments run through MyDose. After costs, the rest is paid to your bank account through Stripe.
Before you start
Your bank account details: routing and account number.
Your business type, EIN and address. Studio asks for them under
Business details
before payout setup.
Connect payouts
Open Payouts
In your sidebar, select
Payouts
, or select
Set up payouts
on your Home checklist.
Follow the Stripe steps
Stripe opens and asks for your details and bank account. Complete every step.
Return to Studio
When Stripe finishes, you return to the Payouts page.
Check your status
Payouts shows as connected when Stripe has all it needs. Home marks
Payouts connected
.
Going live for the first time needs payouts to be ready.
What the Payouts page shows
Tile
Meaning
On its way
Money earned that has not reached your bank yet
Paid out
Money sent to your bank
Test sales
Orders placed in test mode. These are not paid out
Below the tiles is your payout history. See your Payouts page for what is on its way.
What comes out of a payout
Medication and provider consults, at cost. MyDose adds no markup.
Labs, prescriptions and shipping, at cost.
The $15 per active patient above the 50 included in your plan, if you have any. See
Billing and your plan
.
You keep the rest of what the patient paid.
If the amounts taken use up a whole payout, that payout is settled without a transfer to your bank.
Timing
See your Payouts page for what is on its way.
Test mode
Test orders never reach your bank account. See
Test mode and going live
.
What you'll see
Payouts page with On its way, Paid out and business details
After connecting, the tiles show zero until your first live sale. Then On its way fills, and later Paid out.
What happens next
Once payouts are ready you can go live. See
Test mode and going live
.
Common problems
Symptom
Fix
Payouts will not connect
Go through the Stripe steps again. Check bank details.
Go live is blocked
Payouts must be ready. Finish the Stripe steps.
Stripe asks for more information
Open Payouts and complete the item Stripe lists.
My payout is smaller than expected
Costs and any per-patient amount are taken out first. Compare with your orders.
A payout shows no transfer
Amounts taken equaled the payout, so it was settled without a transfer.
