The clinic operating system for protocol-based care
MyDose Clinical Software replaces fragmented workflows with one unified system for intake, protocols, appointments, telehealth, prescribing, and fulfillment. Built specifically for clinics running peptide, hormone, GLP-1, and lab-based longevity programs.
Sign Up for Free
Your EMR was designed for insurance billing and episodic visits. MyDose was built for multi-month protocols with check-ins, lab milestones, and patient tracking over time. It's the platform your team actually wants to use—and the system you can confidently show to regulators, partners, and auditors.
WHAT IT IS
One platform. Every step of protocol-based care.
MyDose Clinical Software is the system your clinic team uses to design, deliver, and track longevity protocols from initial consultation through months or years of ongoing care.
Most clinics running peptide, hormone, or GLP-1 programs are using a Frankenstein stack: an EMR for billing, Google Sheets for protocol tracking, email for patient communication, and hope that nothing falls through the cracks. MyDose replaces that chaos with one unified platform where protocols are structured, patients are tracked, teams are coordinated, and compliance is automatic.
What makes it different from your EMR:
Protocol logic is native, not hacked
Your EMR thinks in “visits” and “billing codes.” MyDose thinks in “protocols” and “timelines.” Want to set up a 12-week peptide program with check-ins at Week 4, labs at Week 6, and a dose adjustment at Week 8? MyDose makes that simple.
Patient tracking over time, not snapshots
Most EMRs show you what happened at the last visit. MyDose shows you where each patient is in their protocol, what they’ve logged since the last check-in, how their labs are trending, and what’s coming next.
Built for teams, not solo providers
Longevity clinics often have multiple providers, front-desk staff, lab coordinators, and patient success teams. MyDose gives everyone the right view: providers see clinical context, staff see task queues, coordinators see fulfillment status.
Telehealth integrated, not bolted on
Video consultations run directly in MyDose. Appointment notes, prescriptions, and protocol adjustments all happen in the same system.
Compliance is automatic, not manual
Every action creates an audit trail. Every protocol step is logged with timestamps. Consent forms are digital and stored securely. If a regulator asks “How did you manage this patient’s care?”, you have a complete, traceable record.

Common questions
1
Can we customize MyDose for our specific protocols?
Yes. MyDose is built to be flexible. You can create custom protocol templates, adjust timelines, define check-in schedules, and customize intake forms. If you need something highly specific, our team can work with you to build custom workflows.
2
Do we have to switch from our current EMR?
Not necessarily. Some clinics use MyDose for longevity and functional medicine programs while keeping their existing EMR for insurance-based care. Others replace their EMR entirely. It depends on your practice mix.
3
How long does implementation take?
Most programs go live in about 14 days. That includes onboarding, protocol setup, team training and the first patient moves. We provide dedicated support during implementation.
4
What if our pharmacy doesn't integrate with MyDose?
We work with a network of 503A U.S. pharmacies. If your current pharmacy doesn't integrate, we can connect you with one that does.
5
Is MyDose HIPAA compliant?
Yes. MyDose runs on HIPAA-compliant infrastructure with end-to-end encryption, secure messaging, role-based access controls, and automatic audit trails. We're also working toward SOC 2 Type II certification.
6
How does Enterprise pricing work?
Pricing is scoped with our team around your patients, programs and setup. Medication is negotiated and passed through with no markup.
