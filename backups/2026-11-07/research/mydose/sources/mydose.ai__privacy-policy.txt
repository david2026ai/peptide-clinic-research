3. COOKIES, TRACKING TECHNOLOGIES, AND ADVERTISING
3.1 What Are Cookies and Tracking Technologies?
Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. Pixels (also called web beacons or tracking pixels) are tiny, invisible images embedded in web pages or emails that transmit information when loaded. Tags and scripts are code snippets that enable third-party services to collect data. Together, these technologies enable us and our partners to recognize your browser, remember preferences, measure advertising performance, and deliver relevant content.
