MyDose Studio overview
What Studio is, what you do, what MyDose handles, and what it costs.
MyDose Studio is self-serve software for running your own telehealth brand. You build a storefront under your own name, choose the products it sells, and connect Stripe to get paid.
What you do
Build your storefront with templates and the visual editor.
Set your brand kit: logo, colors and fonts.
Choose which MyDose products your store sells.
Connect Stripe so payouts reach your bank account.
Find patients and send them to your store.
What MyDose handles
Independent licensed providers review every patient intake and decide whether to prescribe.
A partner pharmacy fulfills and ships orders.
Intake, e-consent, checkout and patient records run on MyDose.
The MyDose care team answers patients' medical questions.
Medical services provided by independent licensed providers. You never make a medical decision. The rules that come with medical services apply to your pages and your ads.
What it costs
$0 until your first patient.
Then $499 a month, with 50 active patients included.
$15 per active patient after 50. The $15 is taken from your payouts, not charged to a card.
No setup fee.

What Studio does not do
It does not send text messages or run marketing sequences.
It does not let you message patients from Studio. Support shows metadata, and replies happen in your MyDose clinic dashboard.
It does not send sign-ups or purchases to ad platforms.
It does not promise patients, results or income.
Is Studio right for you?
Studio is for founders, clinics, med spas, wellness brands and creators who want a healthcare brand under their own name and will do the work of finding patients. It is not a way to make quick money from a trend.
