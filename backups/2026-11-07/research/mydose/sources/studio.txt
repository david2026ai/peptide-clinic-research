5a
Your storefront, your brand kit, your domain.
Pick a template, set your logo, colors and fonts once, and edit your pages from the dashboard. Your storefront starts at yourbrand.mydose.store and runs on your own domain once you connect it.
5b
You set the price.
Medication and provider consults pass through at cost, with no MyDose markup.
5c
Orders, patients and subscriptions in one place.
See every order, patient and subscription as it happens. Refund, cancel, pause or resume from the dashboard.
5d
Analytics and attribution.
Visits, sales and ad results across your stores, as totals (no individual patients), with attribution by source, campaign and landing page. Build tracking links for Meta, Google Ads and TikTok, add your ad spend, and see cost per patient and ROAS.
5e
Ad tracking with patient privacy built in.
Paste a key for GA4, Meta or TikTok and Studio sends visits from your general pages only. Never a name, email, phone number, product, treatment or answer. Patient sign-ups and purchases are never shared.
5f
Payouts and billing.
Connect one bank account through Stripe. Your share of every sale is paid out, and the plan fee comes out of your payouts. Nothing is billed until your first patient.
5g
Team, notifications, more than one storefront.
Invite teammates as Admin or Staff. Get a daily summary and a payment alert for every sale. Run more than one storefront from one account.

FAQ
What do I keep?
Your price minus MyDose’s cost, on every sale. Medication and provider consults pass through at cost. Once you have a live patient, the plan is $499 a month with 50 active patients included and $15 for each one past that, taken from your payouts.
Who is the doctor, and who is liable?
Independent licensed providers review every intake and decide whether to prescribe. They are not your employees, and you never make a medical decision.
Medical services provided by independent licensed providers. Compounded medications are not FDA-approved.
Do I own my patients and my brand?
The brand is yours: the name, the domain, the logo, the pages, the prices. Patients sign up on your storefront and appear in your dashboard with their orders and subscriptions.
Can I try it before I pay?
Yes. Build the storefront, set products and prices, invite your team and run the store in test mode for as long as you want. You pay nothing until your first live patient.
What happens when I go live?
Connect a bank account through Stripe and switch the store to live. From then on patients can sign up, providers review intakes, the pharmacy ships, and payouts start. Billing starts with your first live patient.
Who answers my patients’ questions?
Medical questions go to the care team, through the patient portal, and the care team answers them. You see support conversations in Studio.
Is this for me?
If you want a healthcare brand under your own name, and you will do the work of finding patients, yes. If you want quick money off a trend, no. Medical decisions belong to the providers, and the rules that come with them apply to your pages and your ads.
