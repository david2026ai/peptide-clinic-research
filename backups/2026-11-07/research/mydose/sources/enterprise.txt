Common questions
How long does a migration take?
Switching programs go live in about 14 days once branding, program selection and payment setup are done. Both platforms run side by side and each patient moves at their next refill.
Can we keep our pharmacy relationships?
Yes, where the pharmacy is a licensed 503A partner. Otherwise orders route through MyDose’s pharmacy network.
Can we use our own providers?
Yes. Bring your own medical group and skip the per-patient provider fee, or use MyDose’s licensed providers.
What does Enterprise cost?
Enterprise pricing is scoped with our team. Schedule a meeting to talk through your setup, timeline and terms. Medication is billed at pharmacy cost.
When is Enterprise the right fit over Studio?
When you have live patients to migrate, custom clinical workflows, several brands or channels, or need contractual terms beyond self-service.
