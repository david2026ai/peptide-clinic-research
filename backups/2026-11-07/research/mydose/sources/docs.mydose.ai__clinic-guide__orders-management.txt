Orders Management
Track your clinic's orders and your patients' orders from one page, with search, filters, and sorting.
Orders Management is a single page for tracking ordering activity across your clinic — both the orders your clinic places itself and the orders placed for your patients.
Where to find it
In the sidebar, under the
OPERATIONS
group, select
Orders Management
. If you don't see it, sidebar items are hidden or disabled based on a member's permissions — see
Roles & permissions
.
Orders Management - Clinic Orders tab
The two tabs
The page splits orders into two tabs:
Tab
What it shows
Clinic Orders
Orders your clinic has placed itself
Patient Orders
Orders placed for or by your patients
Both tabs include a
Search
box and filter dropdowns to narrow the list. The
Patient Orders
tab also has a
Newest
sort and an
All
filter.
Orders Management - Patient Orders tab
To find a specific order:
Pick the right tab
Select
Clinic Orders
for your clinic's own orders, or
Patient Orders
for orders tied to patients.
Search or filter
Type in the
Search
box, or use the filter dropdowns. On
Patient Orders
, use the
Newest
sort to bring the most recent orders to the top.
If your clinic hasn't placed any orders yet, both tabs show an empty state — that's expected for a new clinic. The lists fill in as orders are placed.
Orders on the patient profile
Each patient also has an
Orders
tab on their own profile, which holds that patient's order history along with unpaid orders and current delivery tracking. Ordering for a patient starts from the
Order Products
button in the patient profile header — see
Patient orders
for the details.
The
Pharmacy
and
Inventory
sidebar items also relate to fulfillment, but they aren't covered by this guide yet.
