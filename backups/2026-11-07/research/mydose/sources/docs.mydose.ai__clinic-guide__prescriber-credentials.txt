Prescriber Credentials & Profile
Add your NPI, DEA registration, state medical licenses, and e-signature in Account Settings so you can prescribe and activate treatments.
Your prescriber credentials — NPI, DEA registration, and state medical licenses — live in your personal
Account Settings
, along with your e-signature and security options. Keeping them complete and current is what unlocks prescribing in MyDose.

gear icon

The
Licensing
and
E-signature
tabs appear for prescribing team members. Credentials are personal to each account — every prescriber signs in and manages their own from Account Settings, not from Team Members.
Why credentials are required
MyDose uses your credentials to identify you as the prescribing clinician on treatment plans and prescriptions. Until your medical license is verified, you can't prescribe or activate treatments — it's one of the four items in the
GETTING STARTED
checklist on your Dashboard ("Verify medical license — Required to prescribe and activate treatments"). Your e-signature is then used to sign documents and prescriptions inside the platform.

Practice in multiple states
If you treat patients in more than one state, add a separate license entry for every state where you're licensed. Add the license before you start treating patients in that state, and keep your DEA
Registered state
accurate as well. Removing a license entry removes that state from your credential record, so only remove licenses that have genuinely lapsed or that you no longer use.
Keep credentials current
Enter expiration dates carefully and update them whenever you renew a license or DEA registration. An expired credential on file can interrupt your ability to prescribe — set your own renewal reminders well ahead of each expiration date.
When anything changes — a renewed state license, a new DEA registration, an additional state — update the Licensing tab right away and select
Save Settings
so your record stays accurate.
