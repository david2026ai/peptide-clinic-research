Review and Approve Intakes
How to review a patient's intake responses, use the AI summary and review checklist, and approve the intake so treatment can begin.
Before a treatment plan moves forward, a prescriber reviews the patient's intake. This page covers where reviews live, what's on the review screen, and how to approve.
What is an intake?
An intake is a health questionnaire a patient completes as part of their treatment plan or booking flow. It collects the clinical information you need to assess eligibility before prescribing — symptoms, medical history, current medications, and uploaded photos.
Intakes that need your review appear in the
Actions Center
, your queue for open clinical tasks. The badge on the sidebar icon counts the review items waiting for you.

The review screen
The header shows the patient's name and the consultation you're reviewing, with the note "All relevant intake information is summarized below for clinical review." Two buttons sit at the top:
View Full Intake
— opens the patient's complete questionnaire responses.
Approve & Continue
— records your approval and moves the intake forward.
