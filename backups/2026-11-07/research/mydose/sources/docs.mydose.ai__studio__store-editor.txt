Edit your store
Open the visual editor, change a page, then save, schedule and publish.
The store editor is a visual page builder. It opens from your dashboard already signed in, so there is no second password.
Before you start
Your
brand kit
is set and your
products
are chosen.
Your store exists. It is created at the end of the setup wizard.
Open the editor
Go to Storefronts
In your dashboard sidebar, select
Storefronts
, then the store you want to edit.
Select Edit store
The editor opens at your store's admin address in a new tab.
Choose a page
Pick a page from
Pages
, or create a new one.
The editor works while your store is in test mode. A
Sandbox
badge shows at the top right.
Edit a page
Select a block
Click any block on the page. Its settings open on the right.
Change the content
Edit text, images, buttons and links in the settings panel.
Add or reorder blocks
Use the left rail.
Blocks
adds sections.
Outline
shows the order of blocks on the page.
Check every screen size
Switch between desktop, tablet and phone previews at the top.
Save a block to reuse
Use the toolbar above a block to duplicate it, delete it or save it as a reusable section.
More than 80 ready-made blocks are available: heroes, steps, FAQs, pricing, reviews, forms and more, plus sections for disclosures, state availability and provider notices. These are starting points, not legal advice. Have your counsel review your disclosures.
Save, schedule and publish
Action
What it does
Save draft
Keeps your work private. The live page does not change.
Publish
Puts the page live on your store.
Schedule
Publishes at a date and time you pick, in your local time.
History
Keeps every save. Restoring a version brings it back as a new draft.
Trash
Holds deleted pages so you can restore them.
Starter pages
A new store starts with Home, About Us, Contact, FAQ, Terms of Service, Privacy Policy, Telehealth Consent, Refund Policy and Notice of Privacy Practices. The legal pages are starter text. Review them with your own counsel before you go live.
What you'll see
Storefronts page with the Open editor button
A page preview in the centre, blocks listed on the left, settings on the right. After
Publish
, the public page changes.
