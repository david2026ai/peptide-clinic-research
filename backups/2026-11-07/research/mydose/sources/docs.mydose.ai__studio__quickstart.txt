Quickstart
From sign-up to a live store in seven steps.
This is the short path. Each step links to a full page.
Getting started
has the longer checklist.
Before you start
A work email you can open.
A bank account for payouts, ready by step 6.
You can stop at any point. Everything saves as you go.
Create your account
Go to studio.mydose.ai/start and complete About you, Your business, Plan, Verify email and Website. See
Create your account
.
Set up your brand kit
In the setup wizard, complete
Brand
,
Colors
and
Look
. You can change them later under
Brand kit
. See
Brand kit
.
Choose your products
Open
Products
and turn on the MyDose products you want to sell. See
Choose your products
.
Build your pages
Open
Storefronts
, select your store, then
Edit store
. Edit Home, About, Contact and FAQ. Select
Publish
on each. See
Edit your store
.
Check your test store
Your store starts in test mode. A banner says no real orders are taken. Click through it as a patient would. See
Test mode and going live
.
Connect payouts
Open
Payouts
and follow the Stripe steps. The first go-live needs this done. See
Payouts
.
Go live
Select
Go live
and switch the store to live. Patients can then sign up. Your plan starts with your first patient. See
Billing and your plan
.
What you'll see
After step 1 you land in the setup wizard. After step 4 your pages show on
<your-store>.mydose.store
with the test banner. After step 7 the banner is gone and the
Go live
item leaves your sidebar.
What happens next
Share your store link. Watch
Orders
and
Patients
in your dashboard. Read
First patient playbook
.

Symptom
Fix

Troubleshooting
.
Go live is blocked
Finish the Stripe steps under
Payouts
.
My edits do not show
Select
Publish
. Saving a draft does not change the live store.
