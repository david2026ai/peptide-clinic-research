Message Patients
Read and reply to patient conversations, start new chats, and message directly from a patient's profile in the MyDose clinic portal.
Keep patient communication in one place — the
Messages
inbox holds every conversation between your clinic and your patients, and you can also chat inline from a patient's profile.
Where to find it
Select
Messages
in the sidebar (under the CARE group) to open your inbox. Each conversation shows the patient's name, the day of the last message, and a preview — when the last message was yours, the preview is prefixed with
You:
.

Tips for clinical messaging
Keep it actionable.
Short, specific messages ("Your refill ships Monday — take with food") get read; long paragraphs get skimmed.
Attach, don't describe.
Use
Attach image or PDF
for dosing instructions, prep sheets, or forms instead of typing them out.
Check Files & Media first.
Before asking a patient to resend something, check the
Files & Media
tab — it collects everything already shared in the thread.
Set expectations for urgency.
Messaging is asynchronous. Make sure patients know it isn't for emergencies and give them a phone number for urgent issues.
Message from where you're working.
Use the inbox for triaging replies across patients, and the profile's
Chat
card when you're already in a specific patient's chart.
