Compliance for brand owners
What you can and cannot say on your pages and in your ads.
You run the brand. You are responsible for what your pages and ads say. Independent licensed providers make all medical decisions. These rules keep your claims accurate.
Medical services provided by independent licensed providers. Compounded medications are not FDA-approved.
Before you start
Open your pages in the store editor so you can fix wording as you read.
Have your own counsel available. Starter text is not legal advice.
Review your store
Read every page
Open Home, About, Contact, FAQ, product pages and your legal pages. Read as a patient would.
Check the do-not list
Compare the wording against the table below. Fix anything that matches.
Check disclosures
Make sure the disclosure and provider notice sections show where they apply.
Have counsel read your legal pages
Terms of Service, Privacy Policy, Telehealth Consent, Refund Policy and Notice of Privacy Practices are starter text.
Publish and recheck
Select
Publish
, then view the live page.
What to say
Describe what your brand offers in plain words.
Say that independent licensed providers review every intake and decide whether to prescribe.
Show the required disclosure lines where they apply.
Use the pricing and cost wording in
Billing and your plan
.
What not to say
Do not
Why
Say a treatment is FDA approved
Compounded medications are not FDA-approved
Say a treatment cures or treats a condition
Only providers make medical decisions
Promise results or share patient outcomes
Results differ by person
Use brand-name drug names in ads or headlines
Ad platforms and regulators restrict this
Use before-and-after photos
Not allowed on MyDose storefronts
Say your brand has its own doctors or pharmacy if it does not
Providers and pharmacy are independent
Promise a patient will be approved
Providers decide
Promise earnings or speed of growth
Not a claim MyDose can support
Give medical advice in chat, email or social posts
Send medical questions to the MyDose care team
Ads
Ad platforms count page visits only on pages with no health content. Use the
General page (ad landing)
switch only on pages with no health content. It does not promise that a platform will approve your ads. Studio never sends sign-ups or purchases to ad platforms. See
SEO and pages for ads
.
Patient information
Do not put patient information in emails, social posts or support messages. Studio shows totals in Analytics and Marketing, not patient rows.
Patients' questions
Medical questions go to the MyDose care team. Do not answer them yourself. See
Patients, orders and support
.
What you'll see
A clean read-through with no matches in the do-not list, and disclosure sections in place.
