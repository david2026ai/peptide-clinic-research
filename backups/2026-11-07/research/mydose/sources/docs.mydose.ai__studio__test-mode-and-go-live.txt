Test mode and going live
What test mode does, what you need before you go live and what changes after.
Test mode and live mode are set for each store. You can stay in test mode as long as you like. Nothing in it costs money.
Test mode
Your store is published. Public pages show a banner that says no real orders are taken.
Test activity does not send patient emails and does not start your billing.
Test orders are not paid out.
You can build, edit and publish pages.
Before you go live
Check these in your test store.
Your products, pages and menus are as you want them.
Your claims follow
Compliance for brand owners
.
Your legal pages are reviewed by your own counsel. The starter pages are starter text.
Payouts are connected. The first go-live needs this. See
Payouts
.
You clicked through the store as a patient: home page, product page, sign-up and checkout.
Go live
Open Go live
Use
Go live
in your sidebar, or the Go live banner on Home. It shows only while a store is in test mode.
Switch the store to live
Switch the store to live. Patients can then sign up, independent licensed providers review intakes and the pharmacy ships orders.
