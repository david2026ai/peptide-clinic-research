# Research-only handoff rules

- This directory is a research artifact set, not a repository or app. Do not make production changes without a new explicit implementation brief.
- Existing user GitHub/Origin repositories and global/shared skills remain untouched. No connector installations or auth widening under this brief.
- Refresh every material price/regulatory/platform/API fact at runtime from its official source; record dates/version/scope. Do not infer vendor adoption from MCP availability.
- Synthetic data only for initial design/testing. No credentials/PAN/PHI in source, prompts, research files, screenshots or ordinary logs. BAAs for a platform do not automatically cover AI clients/proxies/traces.
- Distinguish recommended architecture from actual MyDose integrations; unknown private contract/API rights are not invented.
- Preserve medical independence, record custody, legal retention and patient continuity. No general-agent prescribing, eRx signing, clinical substitution or record deletion.
- Publication, sends, spending, refunds, access changes and migration require separate exact authorization; professional clinical/legal/privacy approvals are independent.
- Prefer negotiable volume caps and total-cost evidence over an arbitrary patient51migration. Ownfrontend alone does not removebackend fees.
