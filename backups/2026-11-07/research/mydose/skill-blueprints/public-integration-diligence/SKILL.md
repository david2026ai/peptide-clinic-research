# Clinic public integration diligence

DRAFT — generic local authoring artifact; not installed, legal advice, clinical authorization or a guarantee of platform approval.

## Universal boundaries
This is a local draft, not an installed skill or authorization to connect accounts. Refresh official documentation and policies at execution time. Record URLs, access dates, product/version, entity, environment and unknowns. Distinguish observed UI, vendor claim, documented capability, recommended substitute and unverified/private capability. Do not infer another vendor's technology adoption from an available MCP.

Use synthetic data first. No real PHI, credentials, payment tokens or private keys in prompts, screenshots, ordinary logs, repository files or research artifacts. A platform BAA does not automatically cover the AI client, MCP proxy, model provider or tracing. Do not widen access, install connectors, scrape authenticated apps, use undocumented APIs or enumerate hidden tools. Use sanctioned connectors with least privilege only after separately authorized setup.

Internal PASS / REVISE / BLOCKED is not legal approval. Record clinical, legal/privacy, platform and business-publication approval separately. User authorization to research or draft is not authorization to publish, spend, send, refund, alter access, migrate records or prescribe. No general agent may override a clinician or pharmacist. Stop before externally visible or clinical/financial mutations absent the exact required authorization and professional workflow.

## Workflow
1. Identify exact legal entity, product/plan, jurisdiction, clinical services, intended users and owned-versus-vendor assets. Keep unknowns explicit.
2. Start with the public product URL, official pricing, docs index, terms, privacy and BAA references. Public browser fallback only if normal fetch fails; never authenticate or inspect internal app traffic for research.
3. For every integration record vendor legal name; evidence class; actual documented interface/endpoint; read/write scopes; authentication; input/output; source of truth; PHI; owner/payer; plan limits; export/termination; date/source. Demo data and examples are not production offers or guarantees.
4. Distinguish operational MCP, documentation-only MCP, standard API/webhooks and hypothetical custom wrappers. Check official company docs/repo; do not invent an endpoint from product naming or third-party catalog entries.
5. Ask for missing commercial schedules rather than fabricate prices: clinical/pharmacy/lab/shipping/processing, per-patient definitions, minimums, usage, security support, pass-throughs and migration charges.
6. Produce a feature/contract gap list, source register, diligence questions and go/no-go gates. A 404 or private-page barrier is an access issue, not evidence of noncompliance or absence of service.

## Output
Integration inventory CSV/JSON; source citations/access log; known/unknown/alternative labels; exact next documents and tests. Do not contact vendors or create accounts.
