Internal PASS / REVISE / BLOCKED is not legal approval. Record clinical, legal/privacy, platform and business-publication approval separately. Stop before externally visible or clinical/financial mutations absent the exact required authorization and professional workflow.

## Workflow
1. Verify written authorization, medical custodian, legal basis/patient notices and consent, destination coverage, contracts/BAAs, record retention, payment mandates, certification and rollback access. No private UI scraping or unsupported API extraction.
2. Build a migration ledger per authorized patient and plan: old/new clinical IDs, export manifest, consents, clinician/pharmacy responsibility, paid-through/next billing anchor, remaining fills, in-flight shipment, open messages/labs/critical tasks, discrepancies and accountable reviewer.
3. Start synthetic. Then, only after approvals, plan new patients on the new stack before consented established cohorts. An open prescription or clinician relationship is not just a database row; pharmacist/prescriber decides transfer or reissue and whether a new visit is necessary.
4. Customer/payment-method copy is not subscription migration. Use processor-mediated transfer only; map changed IDs; preserve anchors/credits/cancellations. Confirm old collection stops before new billing. Disconnecting a connected account is not proof all subscriptions/application fees stopped.
5. Reconcile charges/refunds, clinical authorization, authorized fills, shipments, labs/messages and audit counts daily during parallel operation. One financial collection owner per obligation and one fulfillment owner per authorized fill; do not suppress legitimate clinician-led changes.
6. Route mismatches to named finance/clinical/pharmacy/privacy owners. Never automatically charge, refund, prescribe, cancel an Rx, alter clinical records or delete records as reconciliation repair.
7. Rollback is a coordinated operational plan: future routing/DNS/software states can change, but a real charge, dispensed medication or clinical encounter is not reversed by database restore. Require clinician-approved continuity and retained source access.
8. Use two full billing/refill cycles without unresolved safety/financial discrepancies as an initial house gate, not a legal standard or universal sufficient duration. Retire operational dependencies only with custodian/legal approval and legally retained access.

## Output
Read-only discrepancy report, ownership ledger, escalation/rollback plan, unresolved safety gates and separately authorized remediation drafts. No live migration execution under this skill.
