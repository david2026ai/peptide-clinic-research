# Clinic platform exit readiness

DRAFT — generic local authoring artifact; not installed, legal advice, clinical authorization or a guarantee of platform approval.

## Universal boundaries
This is a local draft, not an installed skill or authorization to connect accounts. Refresh official documentation and policies at execution time. Record URLs, access dates, product/version, entity, environment and unknowns. Distinguish observed UI, vendor claim, documented capability, recommended substitute and unverified/private capability. Do not infer another vendor's technology adoption from an available MCP.

Use synthetic data first. No real PHI, credentials, payment tokens or private keys in prompts, screenshots, ordinary logs, repository files or research artifacts. A platform BAA does not automatically cover the AI client, MCP proxy, model provider or tracing. Do not widen access, install connectors, scrape authenticated apps, use undocumented APIs or enumerate hidden tools. Use sanctioned connectors with least privilege only after separately authorized setup.

Internal PASS / REVISE / BLOCKED is not legal approval. Record clinical, legal/privacy, platform and business-publication approval separately. User authorization to research or draft is not authorization to publish, spend, send, refund, alter access, migrate records or prescribe. No general agent may override a clinician or pharmacist. Stop before externally visible or clinical/financial mutations absent the exact required authorization and professional workflow.

## Workflow
1. Verify exact live tariff and active-patient counting rules, billing periods, base activation/cancellation, pass-throughs, minimums, extra storefronts, notice and contract version. Separate products and demo plans.
2. Obtain actual MSA/BAA/export schedules; website terms are not enough. Identify medical record custodian, lawful export authority, provider/pharmacy continuity, IP rights, payment account topology and restrictive covenants.
3. Require synthetic full-export acceptance: identity, intakes, questionnaire versions, signed consents/audit, clinical notes, labs/PDFs/units, prescriptions/remaining fills, orders/shipments, messages/files and billing identifiers. CSV import, contact download or a few PDFs is not proof.
4. Compute base and variable fee savings separately. Replacement includes engineering, security/support, cloud, API/platform fees, incremental clinical/lab/pharmacy/processing deltas, record retention, dual running, new consultations and transition churn. Use verified inputs or label assumptions/unknowns.
5. For tariff499+15max(N−50,0), compare full exit against M/T+O+vN. For N>50 and v<15 threshold is N>(O+M/T+251)/(15−v). Own frontend alone does not remove backend fees. If only overage changes while base remains, derive the separate contract-specific model rather than calling it an available hybrid plan.
6. Model growth/ramp, 12/24/36-month horizons, lower/upper recurring replacement costs, payment re-entry/consult costs and net contribution lost from transition churn. No false precision or guaranteed savings.
7. Negotiate volume pricing/cap and portable partner contracts before committing migration. Never split accounts or misclassify active patients to evade fees. Do not delete regulated records to lower charges.

## Output
Tariff ledger, assumption/sensitivity table, portability gates, negotiation alternatives, economic trigger and explicit clinical/legal blockers. No cancellation/export/migration action.
