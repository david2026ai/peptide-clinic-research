# Owned clinic marketing workbench

DRAFT — generic local authoring artifact; not installed, legal advice, clinical authorization or a guarantee of platform approval.

## Universal boundaries
This is a local draft, not an installed skill or authorization to connect accounts. Refresh official documentation and policies at execution time. Record URLs, access dates, product/version, entity, environment and unknowns. Distinguish observed UI, vendor claim, documented capability, recommended substitute and unverified/private capability. Do not infer another vendor's technology adoption from an available MCP.

Use synthetic data first. No real PHI, credentials, payment tokens or private keys in prompts, screenshots, ordinary logs, repository files or research artifacts. A platform BAA does not automatically cover the AI client, MCP proxy, model provider or tracing. Do not widen access, install connectors, scrape authenticated apps, use undocumented APIs or enumerate hidden tools. Use sanctioned connectors with least privilege only after separately authorized setup.

Internal PASS / REVISE / BLOCKED is not legal approval. Record clinical, legal/privacy, platform and business-publication approval separately. User authorization to research or draft is not authorization to publish, spend, send, refund, alter access, migrate records or prescribe. No general agent may override a clinician or pharmacist. Stop before externally visible or clinical/financial mutations absent the exact required authorization and professional workflow.

## Workflow
1. Intake jurisdiction, services, patient segment, clinical entity/provider/pharmacy partners, formulation/approval status, indication, visit/lab requirements and allowed prices. TRT, menopause, obesity and experimental peptides are separate evidence/legal tracks; no fixed business mandate.
2. Build an evidence register per exact claim and SKU: excerpt, implied claim, audience, source/evidence strength, approval/compounded/off-label/unapproved classification, qualifications, review date and reviewer. Compounded does not mean FDA-approved; off-label approved drugs differ from unapproved ingredients. Research labels do not authorize human treatment or advertising.
3. Build patient-needs positioning with truthful limitations and transparent recurring price, quantity, duration, visit/lab/medication/shipping exclusions, cancellation and introductory-to-recurring change. Never equate total mg with monthly supply.
4. Website workflow: brief → copywriting → copy editing → clinical-claims filter → platform filter → client rules → human approval. Video workflow: brief → hooks/storyboard/patterns/script → same filters; do not force website copy patterns into every video.
5. Filter exact excerpts and their proposed rewrites. Distinguish law, platform policy and stricter house rules. Preserve factual education rather than banning all medicine discussion. Testimonial typicality/endorsements, personal health attributes, sexual/fertility claims, before/after, expected outcomes, weight loss and implied guarantees need specific review.
6. Reopen current Meta and Google healthcare/personalized-ad policies. Search eligibility is not YouTube prescription-ad eligibility. Certification is not product/creative approval; generic education must not conceal a restricted destination. No cloaking, research-label workaround or patient-list retargeting.
7. Keep clinical intake, booking, checkout, portal and patient messages outside ordinary marketing pixels/session replay. Name/email/phone plus treatment inquiry can be PHI or consumer-health data. Hashing/CAPI/cookie consent is not automatically lawful deidentification or HIPAA authorization. Review actual payloads, referrers, query strings, error logs and downstream vendors.
8. For analytics use approved aggregate spend and public-page reports; no health audience upload or patient-level health conversion into ad systems. Small-cell suppression alone is not legal deidentification.

## Output
Campaign brief, claims register, exact-excerpt/rewrite/verdict audit, price disclosure check, data-flow review, remaining clinical/legal/platform approvals. No publication or campaign modification. Current policy citations required; illustrative copy is not approved copy.
