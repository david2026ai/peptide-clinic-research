# Clinic brand positioning and shortlist

## Conclusion
Advance Plainvero, Rillstead and Clearbyline as three creative directions, not cleared or selected brands. Build around understandable decisions, costs and next steps—not better-medicine claims.

## Scope
US adult cash-pay is assumed. The numerical map uses one illustrative weight-management scenario with Ro and Mochi to keep face competitors comparable. It does not choose the launch niche. Re-map for TRT or menopause. MyDose/Hedonic are potential attach vendors, not face clinical rivals. No patient interviews or willingness-to-pay evidence.

## Focus and Breaker
Candidate Focus: personal cost-and-next-step decision receipt. Its customer value and competitor gap are unvalidated. Price clarity and ongoing support already exist at rivals. No Tier-1/model-blocked breaker established. Itemization, change notices and response ownership are copyable hygiene. Validate operational delivery before advertising the promise.

## Give-ups
- Experimental-peptide-led master brand [not_icp]: No launch segment or clinical/product path selected. Reallocate: Flexible identity and evidence-grounded service pages.
- Cheapest-vial or guaranteed-total-price promise [capital_trap]: Dose, labs, shipping and care scope change actual spending. Reallocate: Itemization, scenario ranges and change notifications.
- All-category launch and sprawling menu [dilutes_focus]: Bounded pilot after clinical/commercial validation; not a menopause mandate. Reallocate: One understandable journey and support ownership.
- Rebuild clinical backend before demand proof [attach_not_face]: MyDose and medical infrastructure are potential attach partners, not face rivals. Reallocate: Contracts/export diligence and visible patient process.
- Clinical-superiority or predictable-outcome claims [not_icp]: Brand ownership and media expertise do not establish better medicine. Reallocate: Clinically reviewed factual education and evidence register.
- Celebrity longevity and biohacking spectacle [lose_to_specialist]: Hone already advertises biomarker-led longevity breadth; theatre is not a moat. Reallocate: Decision clarity and operational trust artifacts.

## Proof before promise
Confirm actual clinical-practice identity/state footprint; price and refund control; support hours/escalation ownership; clinician-approved plan and complete financial receipt; privacy/data-flow boundaries. Owned media/DR experience transfers as testing discipline, not medical expertise or PI claims/tactics. MyDose branding does not establish superior medicine.

## Narrowing
Choose a territory first, then one lead and one backup. Get live registrar confirmation plus first-year/renewal and premium pricing; counsel exact/phonetic/federal/state/common-law clearance; run 5–10 adult pronunciation/recall/comprehension sessions with no PHI; secure clinical/operational approval of promises. Registered exact-match ownership remains unresolved; adding Health does not cure a name conflict.
