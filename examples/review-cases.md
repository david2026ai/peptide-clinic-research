# Small audit examples / acceptance cases

All examples are fictional. Safer rewrites are **drafts**, not pre-approved ads. Each needs accurate service proof, jurisdiction and platform review. Source IDs resolve in SOURCES.

| Exact risky excerpt or setup | Layer / internal finding | Why | Draft replacement / next step |
|---|---|---|---|
| “Your low T is destroying your sex life.” | PLATFORM S14; REVISE | Asserts viewer health/sexual circumstances; shame/diagnostic implication. | “Men's hormone evaluations with a licensed clinician. View visit details and costs.” Recheck actual Rx pathway. |
| “FDA-approved BPC-157 rebuilds tendons.” | Clinical GUIDANCE/status S04; BLOCKED | False approval implication; unmatched human outcome claim. | Neutral education: “BPC-157 is not an FDA-approved treatment. Human evidence remains limited.” No sales CTA implied or authorized. |
| “Compounded semaglutide is the same FDA-approved medicine for less.” | Clinical S02–S03 + price; BLOCKED | Compound not FDA-approved; identity/equivalence and legal-basis claims unsupported. | Explain actual approval status only after product/legal review; no automatic substitute claim. |
| “Our hormones are natural, so there are no risks.” | Clinical S07/S01; REVISE/BLOCKED if unsupported product | “Natural” doesn't establish safety. | “Discuss treatment benefits, risks and alternatives with a clinician.” Needs actual service proof. |
| “$99/month all-in” with $199 recurring care, separate medication and prepaid term | Commercial truth; REVISE | Misleading initial/renewal/total impression. | State real initial charge, ongoing fee, required prepayment and every material exclusion next to price; do not invent the missing schedule. |
| “I lost 40 lb in 8 weeks. Results vary.” | FTC GUIDANCE S01/S10; BLOCKED pending evidence | Consent and a variability disclaimer don't substantiate benefit/typicality. | House launch alternative: remove physiological story; use verified process education. Later testimonial review needs actual expected-results evidence and permissions. |
| Upload hashed patient emails and medication purchases to enhanced conversions | PLATFORM S19; privacy; BLOCKED | Hashing doesn't cure prohibited sensitive conversion upload. | Keep transmission disabled; design legally/permissibly aggregated internal measurement with privacy review. |
| Rename a medical checkout event “GeneralLead” and send via server | Privacy + PLATFORM; BLOCKED | Substance/context and linked identifiers persist. | Audit payload/context; no transmission pending role/legal/platform permission. |
| “Menopause care appointments — see visit pricing.” | Neutral content potentially PASS within text scope | A service topic is not a diagnosis of the viewer. | Retain if true; destination, licenses, certification, targeting and privacy remain PENDING. |
| “All before-after images are illegal.” | HOUSE-versus-law distinction; REVISE | Overbroad invented universal rule. | “Our launch house rule excludes medical/weight-loss result transformations pending specific review; applicable platform/legal limits are evaluated separately.” |
| “TRT video visits will be available under the same federal rules throughout 2027.” | REG S08; BLOCKED | Current temporary extension ends Dec 31, 2026; later authority not established. | No promise past verified authority; develop clinician/counsel continuity plan. |

## Required acceptance checks for a future maintainer
- Neutral factual education is not automatically blocked for mentioning a medicine, menopause, obesity or sexual health.
- A new unsourced healing/anti-aging claim is not accepted with “may” or a tiny disclaimer.
- Approved product, off-label use and compounded/unapproved product produce different analyses.
- Missing jurisdiction/certification/source is explicit, not silently assumed.
- Every audit includes exact excerpt, usable rewrite/remedy, rule layer/source date, internal status and separate human reviews.
- No sample “PASS” turns on external publication, uploads or spend.
- No copied account identifiers, tokens, tracking code or personal patient data enter the dedicated-repo handoff.
