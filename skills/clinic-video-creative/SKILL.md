# Video creative: patterns, production and learning

Read `../../AGENTS.md`, source register, shared references and campaign brief. Use a **video** workflow, not the website copy structure. Capture exact placement, length, service/product/status, audience awareness, state availability, presenter identity/credentials, evidence, prices, CTA and data-safe destination. No account/tool access or generation service is presumed or widened.

## Workflow 1 — angle and hook batches
Generate a requested, reviewable number of concepts; default six grounded hooks plus three exploratory formats, not a compulsory high-volume quota. Every hook must connect to the same evidence-bounded body and CTA. Provide verbatim first lines, format/visual, why it may resonate, claim IDs and unknowns.

Adaptable patterns (hypotheses, not proven healthcare winners):
- **Process preview:** “What happens during a hormone-care evaluation?”
- **Clinician explainer:** approved or properly credentialed speaker explains an actual clinical distinction without diagnosing the viewer.
- **Cost clarity:** explain care fee versus medication/test cost with real terms.
- **Options literacy:** systemic/local or approved/compounded distinctions with current sources.
- **Preparation:** questions to bring to a clinician visit, without consumer dosing advice.
- **Access/continuity:** show a real appointment or follow-up process, without fake waitlists or guaranteed response times.
Exploratory: licensed nonclinical B-roll, clearly illustrative care journey, moderated clinician Q&A. No fake breaking news, secret cures, envy/sexual performance shame, manufactured eligibility, “one trick,” miracle countdown or competitor malice.

## Workflow 2 — timed script
**15–30 seconds:** 0–3 hook; 3–10 explain the service/question; 10–22 verified process/evidence and material price limits; final seconds one accurate CTA. Do not rush a required disclosure into an unreadable final frame.

**45–120 seconds:** hook → contextual education → distinction/options → verified clinician/process proof → how evaluation works → candid limitations/costs → CTA. Length is determined by clarity and disclosure needs, not a fixed conversion doctrine. No obligatory patient transformation story.

For each beat deliver time, exact voiceover, visual, overlay, claim ID, disclosure and source. Screen text, captions, thumbnail/title, music and cuts can change net impression. An anatomical animation or chart must not imply unproven tissue repair, fat melting or age reversal. Avoid needle/gore shock imagery; ordinary factual treatment education is not automatically banned, but requires context/platform review.

## Workflow 3 — deconstruct and adapt
For an existing asset, identify hook, narrative, speaker authority, proof, CTA, audience and measurement limitations. Extract only transferable structure. Remove unsubstantiated claims even if a previous ad ran. A patient story told by a spouse/“adjacent actor” still needs truthful consented facts, outcome substantiation and privacy review; changing narrator is not a compliance shortcut.

## Workflow 4 — complete production brief
Output concept; full timed script; shot list; talent/credentials/releases; licensed asset requirements; overlays/captions; disclosure display/audio plan; primary text/headline; CTA/destination; variants; test hypothesis; clinical/platform/legal/privacy/client statuses. Use synthetic dummy screens, never actual records or intake. Real patient identity/testimonial requires authorization/releases and typicality review. Actor/spokesperson and synthetic-media disclosures must be accurate and prominent where needed; no fake patient or fake clinician testimony. AI cannot create clinical credentials or evidence.

Translate for comprehension; review every language's meaning, risk/price disclosures and talent delivery. Do not infer ethnicity/sexual/health status for audience lists. Disclosures must be understandable in the asset's language and placement.

## Workflow 5 — controlled variation and learning inventory
Isolate ONE variable where feasible: hook wording, opening visual, presenter, approved geographic wording, or CTA. Other elements remain evidence-consistent. Clinical promises, eligibility or risks are not variables to “push harder.” Record asset/version, concept, source IDs, jurisdiction, approvals, spend, impressions/views/CTR, attended evaluations and cohort contribution only through approved privacy-safe measurement. No fixed legal-lead CPL, ROAS or scale/kill thresholds. Claims of better outcomes cannot be inferred from ad metrics.

No autonomous production uploads, launches, account changes, spending or publication. Deliver drafts and recommendations. Every edit/crop/subtitle/hook variant is re-audited. Use clinical filter → applicable platform filter → client/state review and external release gate.

**Illustrative script, not pre-cleared:** “Hormone care starts with an evaluation, not a promise of a prescription. Our clinicians discuss history, symptoms and options. See visit pricing and availability.” Verify that this exactly describes the actual service before use.
