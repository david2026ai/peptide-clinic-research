# Clinic strategy, positioning and offers

Read `../../AGENTS.md`, `../../SOURCES.md`, both shared references and the clinic-context template first. Do not assume the clinic is menopause-first, has a medical license, operates nationally, has pharmacy access or has a particular budget. Unknowns are explicit. No account access is presumed.

## Step 1 — Research before recommending
Record geography, service lines and clinical partners. Segment separately:
- **TRT:** men with clinically evaluated symptoms/consistent low levels versus general performance seekers; fertility interests, controlled-substance rules and monitoring can materially change service design. Do not equate enclomiphene with testosterone.
- **Menopause:** vasomotor/genitourinary symptoms and other concerns; systemic/local, hormonal/nonhormonal and referral options. Do not make everyone a candidate or insist that a lab “balance score” diagnoses every symptom.
- **Obesity:** clinician-evaluated chronic weight-management care, approved product-specific indications, affordability and longitudinal follow-up. Do not price all brands/routes/tiers as interchangeable.
- **Experimental peptides:** separate molecules, formulations/routes, evidence and lawful access questions. Approved peptide medicines are not equivalent to experimental catalogs. A supplier listing is not clinical evidence.

Create an audience matrix: awareness stage, desired information, legitimate objections, supported offer, unsuitable/complex cases and referral route. An age range can be a marketing hypothesis, not a diagnosis or automatic prescribing threshold. Clinical eligibility belongs to clinicians. Use anonymized consented interviews/public research, not patient charts or scraped health profiles.

## Step 2 — Choose mode
### Strategy decision
Frame the real tension; compare at least two viable niches/models against clinical capacity, regulatory dependency, competition, acquisition constraints and fully costed economics. Recommend one with reasons, counterconditions and confidence. No theatrical authority or invented healthcare track record.
### Offer audit
Separate the evaluation, continuing care, medicines, labs and logistics. Audit promise, proof, effort, time to appointment, out-of-scope/refund outcomes and continuity. Reduce administrative friction without removing clinical safeguards. “Value” is clearer access/options, not a guaranteed physiological transformation.
### Funnel audit
Ad → public information/pricing → minimal availability check → secure intake → clinician evaluation → treatment/referral decision → lawful dispensing → follow-up. Locate drop-offs and consent boundaries. Payment before review must explain evaluation charges versus medication authorization/capture and declined-treatment refunds.

## Step 3 — Brand platform
Output: audience, differentiated positioning, defensible process promise, proof required, voice, visual principles, service architecture and three naming territories. Names are NOT trademark/domain/state-practice-name cleared. Verify credentials and response times before copy. Avoid fear, sexual/body shame, medical conspiracies and youth-restoration promises. Preserve empathy and plain language; uncertainty is not a copy defect to delete.

## Step 4 — Price and competition matrix
Open official current competitor links. Record access date, location/service, visit modality, initial/renewal price, billing interval, advance commitment, medication/lab/supply/shipping inclusions, extra care, cancellation, promo/dose/quantity limits and unknowns. Do not rank clinical quality from ad popularity or price. Unknown local geography means a local audit is pending, not invented.

For a proposed offer disclose consultation price, expected medication/testing ranges where verified, first payment, renewal charge/date, monthly equivalent versus upfront payment, dose/pack variations, minimum term, cancellation/refund and no-prescription outcome. “From” requires a real available qualifying tier and nearby limitations. No “all-in” if material compulsory charges remain extra.

## Step 5 — Economic underwriting
Use matched SKU/quantity/supply quotes only. A vial tier or per-tablet price is not automatically one month. Keep pharmacy COGS, consults, repeat care, abnormal-results work, labs, supplies/shipping, processing/refunds, support/software, CAC, fixed overhead, startup and management fees separate.

`Contribution = collected revenue − attributable product/care/support/processing/refund costs`.
`Operating balance = contribution − acquisition spend − fixed costs − applicable management fees`.
Show first-order, recurring, downside/churn and cost-increase cases. Label provider workloads, retention and CAC as assumptions until measured. Revenue share based on revenue less ads is not profit share if COGS remains undeducted. Set any allowable CAC from measured contribution/payback and cash runway, not generic industry CPL or top-line ROAS. No automatic scale/spend action.

## Step 6 — Output and gate
Deliver a one-page decision brief plus source-backed tables, assumptions, dependencies and launch/stop gates. Clinical, counsel and privacy reviews remain separate. Take the proposed messages through the clinical and platform filters. Do not solve an uneconomic offer by adding unsupported claims, unneeded tests, guaranteed prescriptions or experimental products.

**Example, illustrative only:** “Named clinician follow-up and published visit costs” is a testable positioning hypothesis. “The safest hormones that reverse aging” requires revision/blocking, not an extra testimonial.
