# Longevity video ads

Video sells **the membership and the lab-first plan**. It never names a compound (NAD+, sermorelin, GHK-Cu, peptides by name), because Google and Meta treat those as unapproved pharmaceuticals and the account is the asset at risk.

## Angles that fit the buyer (40–60, health-literate, skeptical)

| Angle | Hook pattern | Proof |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Plan, not product | "Your labs came back. Now what?" | Clinician walks a sample plan |
| Honest clinician | "We tell some members they don't need a prescription." | Real clinician on camera |
| At-home | "No clinic. No waiting room." | Kit to results screen |
| 20-year view | "What do you want your 70s to look like?" | Members 45–60, everyday life |
| Price clarity | "One price. Labs, clinician, plan." | Price card |

## Script format (always)

```
ANGLE: …   LENGTH: 15s 9:16 | 30s 16:9 | 60–90s
0–3s HOOK: [line] / [visual]
3–X BODY: [lines] / [visuals]
CTA: "Start with your labs" / end card
SUPERS: "Results vary. Prescriptions only if clinically appropriate."
POLICY CHECK: pass/fail per longevity-claims-guard
```

## Testing system

Hold the body fixed and test 3 hooks per angle; promote a hook only after ≥ 20 conversions. Report cost per member, not cost per click.

## Production

Batch-shoot 3 clinicians + 6 members over 2 days; capture each angle in 3 lengths; build captions, price cards and end frames as reusable templates (Remotion works well).

## Never

Before-and-after imagery, "years younger" or biological-age results, compound names, comparisons to approved drugs, fear of disease as the hook, or targeting by health condition or customer lists on Google.
