## Hook Framework (5-Second YouTube / 3-Second Meta)

The hook must pass: **stop · qualify · intrigue**. No compound names. No outcome claims.

### Hook types that convert for longevity

**1. The Void Hook** (Executive)
> "Most executives I know have optimized their calendar, their team, their investments — and completely ignored their biology."

**2. The Pattern Interrupt** (Optimizer)
> "Your annual physical says you're healthy. That's the wrong test."

**3. The Confession** (Pre-retiree UGC format)
> "At 57 I had every material thing I wanted. I had no energy to enjoy any of it."

**4. The Reframe** (all segments)
> "The question isn't whether you'll decline. The question is how fast — and what you do about it now."

**5. The Credential Hook** (all segments)
> "I'm a [physician/cardiologist/surgeon]. I spent 20 years treating disease. I finally started optimizing my own health."

### Rules
- Never name a compound in the hook
- Never promise a specific outcome
- Qualify the audience fast (income signal words: executive, founder, serious, invested)
- Intrigue without revealing — the hook earns the next 30 seconds

## Script Structure

### YouTube TrueView (90–120 seconds)

```
[0:00–0:05] HOOK — Pattern interrupt or void hook. Brand visible bottom-left.
[0:05–0:20] PROBLEM — Reactive medicine vs. optimization. Audience pain amplified.
[0:20–0:45] BRIDGE — What a real longevity plan looks like (process, not compounds).
[0:45–1:10] PROOF — Social proof framed around process: "What the consultation felt like."
            UGC-style or provider-led. No specific result metrics.
[1:10–1:30] OFFER — Care evaluation + 12-month plan. Clear, specific, no fluff.
[1:30–end]  CTA — "Start your evaluation at stonewellhealth.com." Logo end card.
```

### Meta 9:16 (30–60 seconds)

```
[0:00–0:03] HOOK — Visual + audio simultaneously. Text super over video.
[0:03–0:15] PROBLEM — Fast. One sentence. Resonate or lose them.
[0:15–0:35] PLAN — What we do differently. Plan not vials. Clinician not catalog.
[0:35–end]  CTA — Single clear action. Logo lockup. URL.
```

### VSL (3–5 minutes, landing page)

```
[0:00–0:30]  Hook + problem agitation (3 pain points)
[0:30–1:30]  Why existing solutions fail (reactive medicine, supplement catalogs, DIY)
[1:30–3:00]  Our solution: clinician + labs + 12-month plan (mechanism, not compound)
[3:00–4:00]  Process walkthrough (what happens in your first 30 days)
[4:00–4:30]  Proof (process testimonials, provider credibility)
[4:30–5:00]  Offer + CTA (evaluation → plan → prescriptions only if appropriate)
```

## Channel Specs

### YouTube TrueView
- Aspect: 16:9 (1920×1080 or 3840×2160)
- Length: 15s (non-skippable), 90–120s (skippable TrueView)
- Targeting: In-market health optimization, affinity avid health enthusiast, keyword contextual (longevity, healthspan, biohacking — content match only, non-Rx creative)
- Brand placement: Lower-left at 0:00, end card at final 5–20 seconds
- Bidding: Target CPA or Max Conversions (after LegitScript + Google cert)

### Meta Video (9:16 primary, 1:1 secondary)
- Aspect: 9:16 for Reels/Stories, 1:1 for Feed
- Length: 15–60s for feed, up to 60s for Reels
- Captions: Always. 85% of Meta video viewed without sound.
- Targeting: 38–65, interests in longevity/biohacking/executive health, broad (no health lookalikes)
- Age gate: 18+ minimum; 38+ preferred for efficiency
- Brand in first frame: logo top-left within 1 second

## LegitScript Phase Gates

**Phase 1 (NOW — no cert needed):**
- Service-level ads: "how care works", "personalized longevity program", education-first
- No drug promotion, no compound names
- YouTube education hooks → evaluation CTA
- Meta: awareness + evaluation CTA

**Phase 2 (post-LegitScript ~4–6 weeks):**
- Meta Rx authorization → hormone service ads (18+, geo to certified states)
- Google Healthcare Cert → drug-adjacent keywords (longevity doctor, hormone optimization)
- Paid YouTube TrueView with clinician-endorsed hooks

**Always blocked:**
- Patient-list targeting / health lookalikes / portal pixels
- Compound names in any ad (even post-cert)
- Before/after transformation imagery

## Competitive Intelligence

**What competitors (AgelessRx, Hims) do:**
- Education-first video → consultation CTA
- Provider-voiced explainers on mechanism (builds pre-search trust)
- General "longevity optimization" framing, not compound-specific

**Our differentiation (casemap A2+A5):**
- Plan not catalog: we give a 12-month clinician plan, they give a vial menu
- Evidence honesty: we say what we know, what we don't, and what happens if rules change
- Executive positioning: they target biohackers, we target executives and physicians
