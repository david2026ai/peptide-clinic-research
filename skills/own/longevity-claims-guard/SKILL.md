## Step 1 — Hard Block List (auto-fail in any public copy or ad)

### Compound names (never in ads or public landing pages)
NAD+, NMN (as drug), sermorelin, tesamorelin, ipamorelin, CJC-1295, GHK-Cu, MOTS-c, BPC-157, TB-500, KPV, Semax, Epitalon, semaglutide, tirzepatide, retatrutide, "peptide stack" naming, any stack brand name

### Outcome words
anti-aging, reverse aging, younger, add years, biological age reduction, cure, prevent [disease], guaranteed, proven to boost, clinically proven [outcome], heals, eliminates

### Forbidden formats
- Before/after photos with health/body metrics
- Patient-result testimonials with specific numbers ("recovered 40% faster")
- "Same as [approved drug]" framing
- "FDA-approved" for any compounded product
- "Research shows" without citing exact peer-reviewed source + date
- Visible vials, syringes, or injection imagery in video

### Platform-specific hard blocks
- **Meta:** Patient-list targeting, health lookalikes, portal pixels, appearance-inferiority copy, fat-pinching imagery
- **Google/YouTube:** Customer Match for health context, sensitive-category remarketing, drug-term keywords without cert
- **Both:** No compound names in keywords, ad copy, or landing page H1

## Step 4 — Placement Rules

- Treatment detail: behind login in patient portal only
- Ad pixels: stop at top-of-funnel marketing pages; NO pixels in intake, labs, checkout, or portal
- TrustedForm: on every landing page variant that collects any lead information
- Clinical intake: separate origin from marketing site — no ad pixels on intake forms

## Step 6 — Output Format

For every piece of copy audited, deliver:

| Line of copy | Issue | Rule (REG/PLATFORM/HOUSE) | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| [exact excerpt] | [what's wrong] | [which rule] | [compliant rewrite] |

**Overall status:**
- **PASS** — no issue found in scope/evidence as of [date]. Not legal clearance.
- **FIX** — remediable with rewrites listed above.
- **BLOCK** — material evidence/authority missing or impermissible channel. Cannot soften.

External review tracks (all start PENDING): clinical, jurisdictional legal, privacy/security, platform certification, client policy.
