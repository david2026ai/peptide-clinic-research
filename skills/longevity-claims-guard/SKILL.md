# Longevity claims guard

Run this before anything public ships. It is a marketing screen, not legal advice; counsel and a clinician sign off on the claims register.

## Step 1 — Block list (hard fail in ads and public landing pages)

Compound names: NAD+, NMN-as-drug, sermorelin, tesamorelin, CJC-1295, ipamorelin, GHK-Cu, MOTS-c, BPC-157, TB-500, semaglutide, tirzepatide, retatrutide, and any "peptide stack" name.
Outcome words: anti-aging, reverse aging, younger, add years, biological age reduction, cure, prevent [disease], guaranteed.
Formats: before/after photos, patient-result testimonials, "same as [approved drug]", "FDA-approved" for compounded products.

## Step 2 — Required elements

- "Prescriptions only if clinically appropriate" (or equivalent) wherever treatment is implied.
- "Results vary" on any member story.
- Compounded medications, when described behind login, labelled as compounded and not FDA-approved.

## Step 3 — Placement

Treatment detail lives behind login in the patient portal. Ad pixels stop at checkout; no pixels in intake, labs or the portal.

## Step 4 — Status refresh

Peptide rules changed several times in 2026 (HHS enforcement-discretion policy Oct 28, 2026 covers only six single-ingredient peptides). Search for the current status before approving any product mention.
