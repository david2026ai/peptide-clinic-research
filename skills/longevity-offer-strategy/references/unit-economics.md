# Unit economics template (per member per month)

| Line | How to compute | Example (Nov 2026 assumptions) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Membership price | set | $229 |
| Lab kits | kits per year × kit cost ÷ 12 | 2 × $295 ÷ 12 = $49 |
| MyDose platform | ($499 + $15 × (actives − 50)) ÷ actives | $12 at 100 actives |
| Clinician reviews | reviews per year × cost ÷ 12 | 4 × $20 ÷ 12 = $7 (verify) |
| Processing | ~3% of price | $7 |
| Support | estimate | $10 |
| Contribution | price − lines | ~$144 |

Payback months = CAC ÷ (contribution + average add-on margin × attach rate).
Always show payback at CAC $300 / $450 / $600 unless real data exists.
